Advanced Guide // 302

Masteries & Account Progression

Level 80 stops raising your character level. Masteries start improving what your whole account can do.

Masteries Mastery Points Account Wide
At a Glance

Masteries in three pieces

Experience

Train the ability

Earn level-80 experience in the appropriate region to fill a Mastery training bar.

Mastery Points

Pay for the unlock

Mastery Points come from things like achievements, story progression, and Mastery Insights.

Unlock

Keep it account-wide

Once purchased, most Mastery abilities become available across your account instead of belonging only to one character.

Core Lesson

Think account upgrades, not character levels

Masteries are one of Guild Wars 2's main forms of horizontal progression.

Instead of turning a level-80 character into level 81, Masteries add abilities and conveniences to your account.

Gliding, mount abilities, auto-loot, fishing, Homestead features, regional traversal tools, and other systems all live somewhere inside Mastery progression.

Think of it like upgrading the operating system instead of replacing the computer.

Part 1 // The Mastery Loop

Train → Earn Points → Unlock

1

Train a Mastery

Open the Mastery section of your Hero Panel and select an available track. Experience earned in the matching region fills its training bar.

2

Collect Mastery Points

Earn points from the same Mastery region through Insights, achievements, story objectives, adventures, and other activities.

3

Spend the Points

Once the training bar is full and you have enough matching Mastery Points, spend them to permanently unlock that ability.

Easy Way to Remember It

Experience trains it. Mastery Points unlock it.

Filling the experience bar does not automatically buy the Mastery. You still need enough Mastery Points from that same region.

If you've filled a bar but can't unlock the next ability, you're probably missing points rather than experience.

Part 2 // Mastery Regions

Points stay with their region

Mastery Points aren't one universal pool. Each major Mastery region has its own points and its own progression.

A Heart of Thorns Mastery Point helps with Heart of Thorns Masteries. It can't be spent on a Path of Fire mount track.

Central Tyria

Core Tyria progression, Pact Commander, Fractal progression, and other core account systems.

Heart of Thorns

Gliding and traversal tools tied heavily to the Maguuma regions.

Path of Fire

Major mount progression and movement abilities.

Icebrood Saga

Regional abilities connected to Icebrood Saga maps and activities.

End of Dragons

Fishing, Skiffs, Jade Bots, Siege Turtle progression, and Canthan systems.

Secrets of the Obscure

Expanded flight tools, Astral Ward progression, and regional mechanics.

Janthir Wilds

Warclaw progression, Lowland Kodan systems, Homesteading, and Janthir-specific abilities.

Visions of Eternity

Castoran Survivalist, Skimmer Adaptation, Wild Castoran Magic, and Castora-related progression.

Don't Memorize This

Follow the Mastery you're actually working on

You don't need to memorize every regional color, track, and expansion before using the system.

If you're working on a Raptor ability, the game will show you the Path of Fire progression you need. If you're working on Gliding, follow the Heart of Thorns track.

Learn the loop first. The regional details will make sense as you encounter them.

Part 3 // What Should I Train?

Prioritize what improves your everyday play

You can eventually unlock everything available to your account. You don't need to do it in one perfect order.

Convenience

Auto-Loot

Pact Commander's Advanced Logistics is a strong quality-of-life goal because defeated enemies can be looted automatically.

Movement

Gliding & Mounts

Movement Masteries make exploration easier and often open routes you couldn't use before.

Content

What you're playing

If you're spending most of your time in one expansion, its Masteries will naturally be more useful.

Personal

What sounds fun?

Fishing, Homesteading, mount upgrades, or another system can be perfectly reasonable priorities if that's what you enjoy.

Veteran Recommendation

Auto-Loot is boring in the best possible way

Some Masteries unlock flashy ways to travel across the world. Advanced Logistics just stops you from manually looting everything.

That sounds less exciting, but it's one of those upgrades you notice every time you play once you have it.

Part 4 // Full Training Bar

What if my Mastery bar is full?

Sometimes you'll finish training a Mastery before you've collected enough Mastery Points to purchase it.

You haven't lost your progress. The trained Mastery stays full until you're ready to unlock it.

Option 1

Train another available track

If another unfinished Mastery is available in that region, switch to it so your regional experience can keep filling something useful.

Option 2

Spirit Shard progress

When no unfinished Mastery track is actively receiving that experience, level-80 experience can instead progress toward a Spirit Shard.

Part 5 // Finding Mastery Points

The game gives you more points than you need

You don't need every possible Mastery Point in a region to complete its Mastery tracks.

If one achievement looks miserable, you can often earn another point somewhere else.

Insights

Explore the map

Some points come from reaching marked locations and communing with Mastery Insights.

Story

Keep progressing

Story chapters and related objectives can award Mastery Points.

Achievements

Follow the point icon

The Achievement Panel can help you find other activities that reward points for the region you're working on.

Why Masteries Feel Good

Your alts benefit too

Mastery progression belongs to the account rather than the character that earned it.

Unlock an account-wide movement or convenience ability once, and your other characters can benefit from that progression too.

Returning Player Check

Haven't touched Masteries in years?

Don't try to reconstruct your entire Mastery history.

1

Open the Mastery Panel

See which regions you've unlocked and which tracks you've already completed.

2

Check what you're currently playing

If you're in Janthir, Cantha, Maguuma, or somewhere else, concentrate on Masteries relevant to that content first.

3

Pick one useful unlock

Don't solve the whole system. Choose one Mastery you actually want and follow that track.

Veteran Field Note

Mastery rank isn't a skill rating

A large Mastery number mostly tells you how much Mastery progression someone has unlocked.

It doesn't automatically tell you how well they understand their profession, how good they are at an encounter, or whether they know the content you're currently doing.

Treat Masteries as account progression, not a scoreboard for player ability.

Try It in Tyria

Follow one Mastery from start to finish

You don't need another checklist. Just use the system once deliberately so the loop makes sense.

Train

Open your Mastery Panel and choose an unfinished ability you actually want.

Find

Look at the Achievement Panel and identify one Mastery Point from the matching region.

Unlock

Once the experience and points are ready, purchase the Mastery and notice what changed on your account.
