Lesson Goal

[ACCOUNT SYSTEM OVERHAUL]

Think of the mastery system as upgrading your account operating system. Hitting level 80 shifts your focus from raw character stats to unlocking permanent abilities like gliders, mounts, auto-looting, and housing across every character on your account.

Module 01

The 3 Step Mastery Loop

Acquiring any account capability follows a clear sequence:

1. Select and Train

Open the Hero Panel (H) to the Mastery tab and choose a track. Experience earned within that region fills the progress bar.

2. Collect Mastery Points

Gather regional points by communing with open world insights, finishing story chapters, or completing achievements.

3. Spend and Activate

Once the experience bar fills, spend your accumulated points to permanently unlock the capability across your whole account.

Pro Tip: Progress is retained automatically whenever you switch between active training tracks.
Module 02

Regional Isolation

Experience and mastery points remain locked to their origin territory:

Central Tyria (Red) Core maps, dungeons, Fractals
Heart of Thorns (Green) Maguuma Jungle, Season 3
Path of Fire (Purple) Elonian Desert, Season 4
Icebrood Saga (Blue) Shiverpeaks, Strike Missions
End of Dragons (Teal) Cantha region
Secrets of the Obscure (Yellow) Horn of Maguuma, Nayos
Janthir Wilds (Orange) Janthir region
Visions of Eternity (Cyan) Castora region

Module 04

Experience Conversion

Experience earned at level 80 never goes to waste:

Overflow Routing

Full experience bars redirect excess progress to the next available untrained track in that region automatically.

Spirit Shard Engine

Fully completing all regional masteries converts every subsequent 254,000 experience points into a Spirit Shard for high tier crafting.
