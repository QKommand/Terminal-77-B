Combo Field & Finisher Reference Guide

How throwing an attack through an elemental zone creates team synergy.

The 4 Finisher Types
  • Blast Finishers: Triggers a large area effect (360 radius) around the blast point.
  • Leap Finishers: Applies an effect to yourself or your target enemy.
  • Projectile Finishers: Shoots an enhanced arrow/bullet through the field.
  • Whirl Finishers: Launches multiple tiny elemental bolts in all directions.
Popular Synergy Examples
  • Fire Field + Blast: Grants nearby allies stacks of Might (+damage).
  • Water Field + Blast: Grants nearby allies instant Area Healing.
  • Smoke Field + Blast/Leap: Grants stealth to drop enemy aggro.
  • Light Field + Any Finisher: Cleanses conditions off you and teammates.
