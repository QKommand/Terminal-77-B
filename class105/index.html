Lesson Goal

[LESSON 5 OF 6]

Master the Tyrian economy, Adventurer. Gold fuels your campaign—from gear stats to commander tags. This course teaches you how to buy gear without overpaying, sell loot efficiently from anywhere on the map, understand listing fees and taxes, and keep gold flowing into your account.

Step 01

Market Access & Buy Orders

Press O anywhere to list or purchase items. Instant Buy pays the lowest current seller price. Custom Buy Orders set your own offered price—use them to save gold on high-value gear and crafting materials.

Open: O Key | Custom Buy = Save Gold
Step 02

Selling & Market Taxes

Instant Sell matches the highest buyer order instantly. Custom Listings match lowest sellers for higher payouts. All trades incur a total 15% tax cut (5% upfront listing fee + 10% sale fee). Never list items below NPC vendor value.

15% Total Cut = 5% Fee + 10% Tax
Step 03

Inventory Liquidation

Salvage fine and masterwork gear for materials, but evaluate rare or exotic items for direct market sales. Liquidate material storage stacks exceeding your 250-unit bank cap to maintain cash liquidity for character growth.

Vendor Floor Limit | Deposit All (250 Cap)

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Remote Market Check: Press O in the open world, search for Iron Ore or Silk Scraps, and place a custom Buy Order below the instant price.
  • Custom Listing Drill: Right-click an unneeded item in inventory, select "Sell on Trading Post", and match the lowest listing price instead of instant selling.
  • Delivery Pickup: Travel to a major hub (e.g., Lion's Arch) and claim your delivered items and collected gold from a Trading Post NPC.
