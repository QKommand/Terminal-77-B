Trading Post

Need something? Someone is probably selling it. Have something you don't need? Someone might be buying it.

Buying Selling Gold
At a Glance

The two choices you'll see a lot

Buy Instantly

Get it now

Pay the current seller price and get the item without waiting. It's convenient, but usually costs more.

Buy Order

Wait and save

Offer the price you're willing to pay and wait for another player to sell the item to your order.

Sell

Fast or patient

Sell immediately to a buyer for speed, or list your own price and wait for someone to buy it.

Core Lesson

Speed costs. Patience usually saves.

The Trading Post is Guild Wars 2's player marketplace. You can open it with the default O key and search for items being bought and sold by other players.

The basic choice is simple. If you want something right now, buy from an existing seller. If you're willing to wait, place a buy order and name the price you're willing to pay.

Selling works the same way in reverse. You can take the best current offer for quick gold or list your item and wait for a buyer.

01 // Buying

Do you need it now?

When you search for an item, you'll normally see what sellers are asking and what buyers are offering.

Buy Instantly

Purchase from the cheapest available sell listing. Use this when convenience matters more than squeezing out every copper.

Place a Buy Order

Put your gold aside at the price you're willing to pay. Your order completes when another player sells to it.

02 // Selling

Do you need the gold now?

Right-clicking many tradeable items lets you open them directly in the Trading Post and see what they're currently worth.

Sell Instantly

Sell directly to the best existing buy order. It's fast, but you're accepting what buyers are currently offering.

List Your Item

Choose a selling price and wait. This may earn you more than an instant sale, but there's no guarantee it sells immediately.

Trading Post Fees

The listed price isn't all yours

The Trading Post takes fees when you sell something. That's worth remembering when you're deciding whether an item is actually worth listing.

5%

Listing Fee

Paid when you put the item up for sale. This fee is not returned if you later cancel the listing.

10%

Exchange Fee

Taken from the sale when the transaction completes.

15%

Total Seller Cost

A completed sale effectively loses 15% of its listed value to Trading Post fees.

What Should I Sell?

Don't turn every drop into a market decision

You don't need to price-check every blue sword that falls into your inventory. Most ordinary equipment can follow the cleanup habits from Archive 103.

Materials

Easy to check

Crafting materials often have an active market. If your storage is overflowing or you need gold, check what they're selling for.

Unusual Drops

Check before salvaging

If something is exotic, unfamiliar, or looks valuable, check its Trading Post price before automatically breaking it down.

Junk

Vendor it

Items meant as vendor junk don't need a market strategy. Sell them to a merchant and move on.

Delivery

Buying something doesn't put it straight in your bag

Purchases and gold from completed sales go into your Trading Post delivery box.

To actually collect those items or coins, visit a Trading Post representative or another source that gives you pickup access.

Don't Panic

Bought something and can't find it in your inventory? Check your Trading Post delivery box before assuming it disappeared.

Veteran Field Note

A few copper isn't worth turning the game into a spreadsheet

Buy orders and sell listings can save or earn you more gold, but sometimes the difference is tiny.

If you're buying a cheap item you need right now, paying a little extra may be worth it. Save the serious price comparisons for expensive gear, large material orders, or anything where the difference actually matters.

Try It in Tyria

Take a look at the market

You don't need to become a trader. Just get comfortable reading the two sides of the market.

Search

Open the Trading Post and search for a common crafting material you recognize.

Compare

Compare the instant purchase price with the current buy orders. Notice how patience changes the price.

Check

Right-click a tradeable item you don't need and look at its market value before deciding whether to sell, keep, or salvage it.
