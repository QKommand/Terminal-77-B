FIELD BRIEFING // TRADING POST BASICS & COMMERCE

MASTER THE TYRIAN ECONOMY, ADVENTURER

Gold fuels your campaign—from gear stats to commander tags. The Black Lion Trading Post is a global marketplace that allows you to buy gear without overpaying and sell field loot instantly from anywhere in Tyria.

Use this field manual to master custom buy and sell orders, calculate market taxes, establish gold-making habits, and keep currency flowing into your account.

MODULE 1: THE MARKET INTERFACE & ACCESS ▼

Remote Access Protocols

You can list items for sale or place buy orders from anywhere in the world. However, collecting physical items or liquid gold requires visiting a local Trading Post NPC or using a portable express item.

MODULE 2: BUYING MECHANICS (INSTANT VS. CUSTOM ORDER) ▼

Purchasing Strategy

Choosing between instant fulfillment and custom offers determines whether you save gold or save time.

MODULE 3: SELLING MECHANICS & MARKET TAXES ▼

Tax Structure & Listing Rules

Every completed market trade incurs a combined 15% tax cut. Understanding this math prevents accidental gold loss.

MODULE 4: GOLD-MAKING HABITS & INVENTORY CLEANUP ▼

Liquidation Protocols

Convert raw materials, dyes, surplus gear, and unopened bags into gold stacks to fund character growth.
