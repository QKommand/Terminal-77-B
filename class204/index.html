Lesson Goal

[ADVANCED TRACK]

Master strike encounters, damage windows, and active combat reflexes. Think of boss fights like a choreographed dance: learn the telegraphs, move with purpose, and strike during vulnerable windows.

Module 01

Telegraph Recognition

Read red attack circles, boss cast animations, and audio cues before major impact hits. Anticipation prevents panic rolling.

Key: Watch boss animations over health bars
Module 02

Damage Windows & Burst

Hold high-impact burst skills until an enemy's breakbar is shattered or a vulnerability window opens for maximum damage output.

Key: Align burst skills with staggered phases
Module 03

Active Mitigation

Use dodges, blocks, and invulnerability frames defensively instead of wasting DPS uptime on unnecessary movement.

Key: Time evades precisely for heavy hits
Module 04

Strike Mission Basics

Transition smoothly from open-world events into structured 10-player instanced boss encounters with coordinated squads.

Key: Coordinate roles in 10-player squads

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Dodge 3 consecutive boss telegraphs successfully without taking damage.
  • Hold your major burst skills until an enemy's breakbar or vulnerability window opens.
  • Complete your first public Strike Mission (e.g., Shiverpeaks Pass).
