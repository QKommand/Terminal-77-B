About Terminal 77-B

A guide made by people who play the game.

Terminal 77-B is a Guild Wars 2 community resource written by members of [Liar]. We wanted somewhere players could look things up without having to dig through a dozen tabs or feel like they needed to study the game before they could enjoy it.

It's especially aimed at new players, returning players, and anyone who's ever looked at something in Guild Wars 2 and thought, “Okay... what does that actually mean?”
