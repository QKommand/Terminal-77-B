Module 01

Understanding Fractals

Fractals of the Mists are short, repeatable five-player dungeons. Each fractal has its own map, story, enemies, and mechanics. Your party chooses a difficulty scale from 1 to 100 before entering.

Personal Fractal Level

Complete a fractal at your current personal level or higher to increase your account-wide personal fractal level by one.

Difficulty Tiers

T1 covers scales 1-25, T2 covers 26-50, T3 covers 51-75, and T4 covers 76-100.

Mistlock Observatory

The lobby contains the scale selector, vendors, fractal potions, infusions, training tools, and the portal used to begin a run.

GENERAL RULE:

There are no normal waypoints inside a fractal. Defeated players must be revived or wait until the party leaves combat before returning to the latest checkpoint.

Module 02

Agony, Resistance, and Tier Scaling

Agony begins at scale 20. It reduces incoming healing and deals a percentage of your maximum health as repeated damage. Agony Resistance, usually added through infusions in Ascended or Legendary equipment, reduces that damage.

Agony Resistance

AR does not make you generally tougher. It specifically reduces Agony damage. Check the selected scale before entering so your resistance meets the requirement.

Mistlock Instabilities

Instabilities are rotating modifiers that change how enemies or players behave. Read them before the run because the same fractal can feel very different from one day to the next.

Module 04

Fractal Combat Fundamentals

Fractals use a lot of shorthand. These terms describe the tools that keep a group alive and make difficult encounters manageable.

Boons

Boons are positive effects. Might increases damage, Fury raises critical chance, Protection reduces damage, Quickness speeds up skill use, Alacrity shortens recharge time, and Stability prevents many control effects.

Boon Strip

Boon strip removes positive effects from enemies. If an enemy gains Protection, removing it restores your party's normal damage. It is especially useful with the No Pain, No Gain instability.

Boon Corruption

Boon corruption removes an enemy boon and replaces it with a harmful condition. It does two jobs at once: the enemy loses a benefit and gains a weakness.

Conditions

Conditions are harmful effects such as Burning, Bleeding, Poison, Chill, Cripple, Immobilize, Fear, Torment, and Confusion. Some deal damage while others limit movement or skill use.

Condition Cleanse

A condition cleanse removes harmful conditions from you or your allies. If your health is falling quickly, check whether Burning, Poison, Bleeding, or another condition is active.

Crowd Control (CC)

Crowd control disables or moves enemies. Daze, Stun, Knockback, Pull, Launch, Fear, Float, and Knockdown all count as CC and damage a boss's blue defiance bar.

Defiance Bar

The blue bar beneath a boss is its defiance bar, often called a breakbar. When it appears, use CC. Breaking it interrupts dangerous attacks and usually creates a safer damage window.

Projectile Defense

Projectile block stops arrows, bullets, and magical shots. Projectile absorb removes them. Projectile reflect sends them back at the attacker and can turn enemy damage into your own.

Stability

Stability prevents many knockbacks, pulls, launches, knockdowns, fears, and other control effects. It does not reduce damage. Think of it as keeping your feet planted.

Adapt Your Build

A single build will not solve every encounter. Swap utility skills when the group needs more cleanse, Stability, boon strip, projectile defense, or CC.

FIELD NOTE:

Damage matters, but preparation is the sharper blade. A party that brings the correct tools will often outperform a party that only brings higher numbers.

Module 05

Mistlock Instabilities

Mistlock Instabilities are rotating modifiers that change the rules of a fractal. They begin in Tier 2, increase to two in Tier 3, and increase to three in Tier 4. Read them before entering because the correct response may be more important than raw damage.

Adrenaline Rush

Enemies deal much more damage while low on health, but they cannot critically hit. Finish weakened enemies quickly and avoid letting several low-health enemies attack at once.

Afflicted

Enemies apply random damaging conditions. Bring condition cleanse and avoid allowing Burning, Bleeding, Poison, or Torment to build up.

Boon Overload

Each boon lowers your maximum health, while your outgoing boon duration is increased. Keep useful boons, but avoid covering the party in unnecessary boons when survival is already difficult.

Flux Bomb

A bomb periodically appears beneath a player and leaves a damaging field. Move it away from the group before it drops. Enemies standing in the field can also receive conditions.

Fractal Vindicators

Fractal Avengers are replaced by stronger Vindicators when players are downed. Revive quickly, interrupt their finishing attacks, and kill active Vindicators before they overwhelm the group.

Frailty

Players take more damage but move faster. Use the extra movement to avoid attacks, because mistakes are punished more heavily.

Hamstrung

Low health slows your movement, while endurance regenerates faster. Dodge early and recover health before the slow makes escaping dangerous attacks difficult.

Last Laugh

Enemies explode when they die unless they were stunned. Move away from defeated enemies, or use crowd control before the finishing blow.

Mists Convergence

Creatures and hazards from other fractals may appear. Stay alert, identify the unexpected threat, and deal with it without losing control of the main encounter.

No Pain, No Gain

Enemies gain boons when struck. Bring boon strip or boon corruption so Protection, Might, and other boons do not make enemies harder to kill.

Outflanked

Nearby allies take much more damage when attacked from behind. Face dangerous enemies toward the same direction and avoid surrounding them carelessly.

Social Awkwardness

Players push one another away when standing too close. Spread slightly, avoid narrow ledges, and use Stability when the group must stack tightly.

Stick Together

You take more damage when far from allies and deal slightly more damage while near them. Stay within roughly 300 range of at least one teammate.

Sugar Rush

Enemies move and attack faster. Watch animations carefully, react sooner, and avoid assuming normal attack timing.

Toxic Sickness

A player periodically vomits in a cone and can hit allies with conditions. Turn away from the group when the warning appears.

Toxic Trail

Enemies leave poison trails behind them. Avoid standing in the trail, and block the trail attack when possible to absorb it and help expose nearby enemies.

Vengeance

When an enemy dies, nearby enemies gain several boons. Focus targets carefully and use boon strip to weaken the survivors.

INSTABILITY CHECK:

Before entering, identify the mechanic, name the counter, and make one small build change. Treat instabilities like weather: you cannot remove the storm, but you can dress for it.
