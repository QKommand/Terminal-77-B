Choosing Your Tyrian Identity

Picking a profession isn't about finding the one perfect class. It's about finding the kind of fight that sounds fun to you.

Professions Playstyle Specializations
At a Glance

Start with the feel, not the math

Heavy Armor

Soldiers

Guardian, Revenant, and Warrior. Big weapons, frontline presence, strong class mechanics, and plenty of ways to protect yourself or your group.

Medium Armor

Adventurers

Engineer, Ranger, and Thief. Gadgets, pets, mobility, tricks, and flexible ways to approach a fight.

Light Armor

Scholars

Elementalist, Mesmer, and Necromancer. Magic-heavy professions with very different ways of controlling the battlefield.

Core Lesson

Pick the fantasy that makes you want to log in

Every Guild Wars 2 profession can be built in different ways. Damage, support, survivability, ranged combat, melee combat, and group utility aren't locked to one armor type.

So don't worry too much about picking the “best” profession. Start with what sounds fun. You can make more characters later, and elite specializations can change how a profession feels in pretty dramatic ways.

If one of the battle descriptions below makes you think “Yeah, I want to do that”, that's a good place to start.

Heavy Armor // Soldier

🛡️ Guardian

Virtues: Justice // Resolve // Courage
Vibe: Paladin // Team Protector // Holy Smiter
Picture Yourself in Battle

You jump into the middle of a fight wrapped in blue and golden magic, block attacks for your team, burn enemies with holy fire, and pull off a clutch save when things start going bad.

How It Plays

Guardians use Virtues, symbols, blocks, and defensive magic while still hitting hard. They naturally lean into protecting allies, burning enemies, and controlling dangerous moments in a fight.

Specialization Options

  • Dragonhunter: Longbow, traps, ranged pressure, and powerful control.
  • Firebrand: Magical tomes, mantras, burning, healing, and strong group support.
  • Willbender: Fast martial movement, aggressive dashes, and close-range pressure.
  • Luminary: Radiant Forge abilities built around weapons of light, damage, and protection.
Pick Guardian if you want: holy fire, strong team defense, and big clutch saves.
Heavy Armor // Soldier

🌀 Revenant

Legends // Energy
Vibe: Mists Knight // Hero Channeler // Stance Swapper
Picture Yourself in Battle

You channel legendary figures from Tyria's past, swap between them in the middle of battle, and completely change what your character is doing without changing professions.

How It Plays

Revenants use Energy to power their skills and swap between two chosen Legends. Each Legend brings its own utility skills and personality, so learning when to switch is a big part of the class.

Specialization Options

  • Herald: Channel Glint and provide powerful boons to yourself and allies.
  • Renegade: Channel Kalla, use a shortbow, and summon members of a ghostly warband.
  • Vindicator: Greatsword combat, legendary alliance abilities, and a powerful leap-style dodge.
  • Conduit: Channel Razah and lean deeper into spirit-based combat and shifting forms.
Pick Revenant if you want: energy management, swapping playstyles, and strong Mists lore.
Heavy Armor // Soldier

⚔️ Warrior

Adrenaline // Burst Skills
Vibe: Weapon Master // Frontline Bruiser // Unstoppable Force
Picture Yourself in Battle

You wade into the front line with a huge weapon, build Adrenaline with every hit, then cash it in on a powerful burst attack that changes the momentum of the fight.

How It Plays

Warrior is direct, physical, and flexible. You get a huge weapon selection, high durability, and Burst skills powered by Adrenaline.

Specialization Options

  • Berserker: Enter Berserk mode and unleash rapid Primal Bursts.
  • Spellbreaker: Strip boons, duel enemies, and punish attacks with Full Counter.
  • Bladesworn: Mix martial combat with a Gunsaber and massive Dragon Slash attacks.
  • Paragon: Use spear, shield, chants, and commands to support allies while staying in the fight.
Pick Warrior if you want: raw physical power, lots of weapons, and heavy hits.
Medium Armor // Adventurer

⚙️ Engineer

Tool Belt // Kits
Vibe: Tech Specialist // Gadgeteer // Invention Master
Picture Yourself in Battle

You swap from grenades to flamethrowers, drop gadgets around the battlefield, fire off Tool Belt skills, and always seem to have one more weird invention ready.

How It Plays

Engineers use gadgets, kits, turrets, elixirs, and Tool Belt skills. They can feel simple or extremely busy depending on how many tools you decide to bring.

Specialization Options

  • Scrapper: Hammer combat, gyros, barrier, and strong close-range utility.
  • Holosmith: Enter the Photon Forge and fight with hard-light weapons while managing heat.
  • Mechanist: Fight beside a large Jade Mech companion.
  • Amalgam: Bond with a strange ooze and use Morph and Evolve abilities.
Pick Engineer if you want: gadgets, flexible toolkits, robots, and lots of buttons to experiment with.
Medium Armor // Adventurer

🏹 Ranger

Pets // Nature
Vibe: Marksman // Beast Tamer // Nature Guardian
Picture Yourself in Battle

Your pet charges ahead while you fire from range. When the enemy closes in, you swap weapons, fight beside your companion, and call on nature to keep the pressure going.

How It Plays

Rangers fight alongside animal companions found throughout Tyria. Pets add damage, control, utility, and personality while you decide whether to fight from range or get up close.

Specialization Options

  • Druid: Staff, Celestial Avatar, healing, and strong group support.
  • Soulbeast: Merge with your pet and gain abilities from its archetype.
  • Untamed: Shift unleashed power between yourself and your pet for aggressive control.
  • Galeshot: Use wind magic and specialized archery to control enemies from range.
Pick Ranger if you want: pets, bows, nature magic, and a strong outdoorsy class fantasy.
Medium Armor // Adventurer

🗡️ Thief

Initiative // Steal
Vibe: Shadow Assassin // Nimble Acrobat // Sniper
Picture Yourself in Battle

You teleport through shadow, disappear into stealth, unload several quick attacks in a row, then slip away before the enemy can pin you down.

How It Plays

Thieves use Initiative instead of normal weapon-skill cooldowns, letting you repeat the skills you need while managing a shared resource. Mobility, stealth, dodges, and shadowsteps are a big part of staying alive.

Specialization Options

  • Daredevil: Extra dodging, staff combat, and highly mobile melee gameplay.
  • Deadeye: Mark targets, build Malice, and fight like a deliberate sniper.
  • Specter: Shadow magic, barrier, ally support, and Shadow Shroud.
  • Antiquary: Fight alongside a Skritt helper and use risky Double Edge skills.
Pick Thief if you want: stealth, shadow movement, quick attacks, and high mobility.
Light Armor // Scholar

🌋 Elementalist

Fire // Water // Air // Earth
Vibe: Spell Caster // Element Dancer // Elemental Wizard
Picture Yourself in Battle

You rain fire on a group, switch to Air for speed and lightning, move into Water when your team needs help, then use Earth when you need control or defense.

How It Plays

Elementalists swap between four elemental attunements instead of swapping weapons normally in combat. That gives you a lot of skills and rewards players who enjoy changing tools constantly.

Specialization Options

  • Tempest: Overload your attunements and lean into powerful elemental support.
  • Weaver: Blend two elements together for fast, layered combat.
  • Catalyst: Use a Jade Sphere and combo fields while fighting with a hammer.
  • Evoker: Work with elemental familiars and charge empowered elemental attacks.
Pick Elementalist if you want: flashy magic, lots of skills, and fast-paced decision making.
Light Armor // Scholar

🔮 Mesmer

Illusions // Shatters
Vibe: Trickster Duelist // Illusionist // Time & Space Mage
Picture Yourself in Battle

Copies of you fill the battlefield, purple magic flashes everywhere, and just when the group thinks they're stuck, you open a portal and move everyone somewhere they shouldn't be able to go.

How It Plays

Mesmers create clones and phantasms, then use Shatter skills to turn those illusions into offense or defense. They also bring some of the game's most unusual utility through portals and other tricks.

Specialization Options

  • Chronomancer: Manipulate time, cooldowns, and Continuum Split.
  • Mirage: Dodge and attack through ambushes while playing like a slippery duelist.
  • Virtuoso: Replace traditional clones with floating psionic blades.
  • Troubadour: Shift toward musical magic, notes, instruments, and ally support.
Pick Mesmer if you want: illusions, portals, time tricks, and purple magic everywhere.
Light Armor // Scholar

💀 Necromancer

Life Force // Shroud
Vibe: Dark Sorcerer // Minion Master // Durable Caster
Picture Yourself in Battle

Enemies fall around you and feed your Life Force. When things get dangerous, you enter Shroud, gain a new set of abilities, and keep fighting while dark magic spreads through the battlefield.

How It Plays

Necromancers build Life Force and spend it through their Shroud mechanic. They tend to feel durable, forgiving, and comfortable in messy fights while still bringing conditions, control, minions, or support depending on the build.

Specialization Options

  • Reaper: Greatsword, close-range pressure, and a powerful scythe-themed Shroud.
  • Scourge: Sand Shades, conditions, barriers, and strong support tools.
  • Harbinger: Pistol, elixirs, high mobility, and aggressive risk-versus-reward gameplay.
  • Ritualist: Ritualist's Shroud, ancestral spirits, and weapon-spell style abilities.
Pick Necromancer if you want: dark magic, strong survival, Shroud gameplay, and undead flavor.

Veteran Field Note

Your first profession doesn't have to be your forever profession

Guild Wars 2 is very alt-friendly. If a profession looks fun, try it. You don't need to solve your entire account before creating your first character.

Even better, elite specializations can make the same profession feel completely different later on.

Try It in Tyria

Pick three, then narrow it down

Don't stare at all nine professions until they blur together. Pick the three that sound coolest and compare those.
