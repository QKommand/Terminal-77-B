Heavy Armor (Soldier)

🛡️ GUARDIAN

Core Mechanic: Virtues (Justice, Resolve, Courage)
Vibe: Paladin, Team Protector, Holy Smiter
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You stand in the middle of a chaotic fight. A wave of fiery arrows flies at your team, so you drop a glowing spirit wall that blocks them all. You leap right into the crowd, bursting into golden flames and keeping your allies healed while you fight."

How It Plays

Guardians use divine Virtues to protect allies and burn enemies. You might have a slightly lower base health pool, but you stay alive easily through constant passive healing, shield blocks, and defensive ground symbols.

Specialization Options

  • Dragonhunter: Grab a Longbow and set traps. You pull enemies in with light spears and blast them from range.
  • Firebrand: Swap your virtues for magical Tomes. You use an axe and chant mantras to burn foes or heal squadmates.
  • Willbender: A fast martial artist in heavy plate. You dash around with dual swords and leave fire tracks on the ground.
  • Luminary: Enter the Radiant Forge to use weapons made of pure light, burning enemies and shielding allies with holy radiance.
👉 Pick Guardian if you want: Glowing holy fire, high team defense, and big clutch saves.
Heavy Armor (Soldier)

🌀 REVENANT

Core Mechanic: Legend Channeling & Energy
Vibe: Mists Knight, Hero Channeler, Stance Swapper
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"Pulsing with Mists magic, you switch stances mid-fight. One second you channel Mallyx to pull bad conditions off teammates and throw them onto enemies. The next second, you switch to Shiro, popping behind a dragon to unleash a lightning-fast flurry of sword strikes!"

How It Plays

Revenants channel legendary figures from history. Instead of standard skill cooldowns, your abilities cost Energy. Swapping between your two chosen Legends resets your Energy bar so you can keep going.

Specialization Options

  • Herald: Channel the dragon Glint to pulse constant speed, damage, and crit buffs to your group with an off-hand Shield.
  • Renegade: Channel the Charr rebel Kalla to fire shortbow arrows through Mists portals and summon ghost warbands.
  • Vindicator: Channel two rival heroes at once. You swing a heavy Greatsword and turn your dodge into a giant leap attack.
  • Conduit: Channel Razah to deepen your bond with spirits, using cosmic energy to unlock new fight forms.
👉 Pick Revenant if you want: Energy management, shifting playstyles mid-fight, and cool spirit lore.
Heavy Armor (Soldier)

⚔️ WARRIOR

Core Mechanic: Adrenaline & Weapon Mastery
Vibe: Weapon Master, Frontline Bruiser, Unstoppable Force
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You slice through the front line with a big Greatsword, building up rage with every swing. Once your bar is full, you unleash a massive attack that knocks enemies down and rallies your team with a loud battle cry!"

How It Plays

Warriors are direct physical fighters. They have high base health, heavy armor, and access to more weapon choices than anyone else. You build Adrenaline by landing hits, then spend it on huge Burst attacks.

Specialization Options

  • Berserker: Go into pure rage mode, swinging off-hand Torches and smashing enemies with rapid-fire Primal Bursts.
  • Spellbreaker: Dual-wield daggers to strip magic off enemies, using Full Counter to absorb incoming hits and strike back.
  • Bladesworn: Use technology and swords together. Charge up your Flow bar to draw a Gunsaber and deliver a massive Dragon Slash.
  • Paragon: Grab a spear and shield, using chants and commands to inspire allies with barrier shields.
👉 Pick Warrior if you want: Raw physical power, tons of weapon choices, and heavy strikes.
Medium Armor (Adventurer)

⚙️ ENGINEER

Core Mechanic: Tool Belt & Engineering Kits
Vibe: Tech Specialist, Gadgeteer, Invention Master
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You pull out a Flamethrower to burn a group of bad guys, instantly swap to toss grenades over a wall, drop a healing turret, and blast off with Rocket Boots without waiting for weapon cooldowns."

How It Plays

Engineers use tech gadgets instead of spells. Rather than standard weapon swaps, you carry utility Kits like Grenades, Bombs, or Flamethrowers alongside a Tool Belt that adds extra skill buttons above your bar.

Specialization Options

  • Scrapper: Swing a Hammer and build flying Gyro drones to speed up allies and revive downed friends from afar.
  • Holosmith: Activate the Photon Forge to craft hard-light weapons made of solid sunlight, burning foes with high heat.
  • Mechanist: Command your own giant Jade Mech battle-bot that tanks, shoots lasers, and fights right beside you.
  • Amalgam: Bond with a strange ooze mold, using Morph skills to change form and trigger powerful Evolve bursts.
👉 Pick Engineer if you want: Flexible toolkits, futuristic gadgets, and fast button pacing.
Medium Armor (Adventurer)

🏹 RANGER

Core Mechanic: Companion Pets & Wilderness Survival
Vibe: Marksman, Beast Tamer, Nature Guardian
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"From way across the field, you rain arrows down with your Longbow while your pet tiger jumps at the target. When an enemy gets close, you swap to a Greatsword, dodge their attack, and call on nature to heal your team."

How It Plays

Rangers fight alongside tame animal companions. You tame pets all across the world, swapping between two in combat to deal extra damage, stun enemies, or shield your party.

Specialization Options

  • Druid: Transform into a Celestial Avatar using a Staff and Glyphs to provide big team heals and offensive buffs.
  • Soulbeast: Merge spirits with your pet to absorb its stats, gaining unique skills while fighting up close with Daggers.
  • Untamed: Juggle raw power back and forth between you and your pet, using a Hammer to control the battlefield.
  • Galeshot: Master wind magic and Tengu archery with a Cyclone Bow, pinning foes down with gust-infused arrows.
👉 Pick Ranger if you want: A loyal pet, long-range archery, or green nature magic.
Medium Armor (Adventurer)

🗡️ THIEF

Core Mechanic: Initiative & Steal
Vibe: Shadow Assassin, Nimble Acrobat, Sniper
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You teleport through shadow, appearing right behind an enemy. You blend into stealth, line up a huge hit, and step away before they know what happened. Low on health? Steal an item straight from your target to flip the fight."

How It Plays

Thieves don't wait on standard skill cooldowns. Instead, you use Initiative, a fast-refilling energy pool that lets you use your favorite weapon skills several times in a row. You stay safe using stealth, dodges, and shadow teleports.

Specialization Options

  • Daredevil: An agile martial artist with three dodge bars. You vault over attacks and smash foes with a melee Staff.
  • Deadeye: A sniper with a Rifle. Kneel down, mark your target, build up Malice, and land a massive stealth headshot.
  • Specter: A shadow mage using a Scepter. Tether your shadow to an ally to give them huge barrier shields, then enter Shadow Shroud.
  • Antiquary: Team up with a Skritt assistant! Swipe rare items off foes and use risky Double Edge skills.
👉 Pick Thief if you want: Shadow teleports, stealth gameplay, and super fast movement.
Light Armor (Scholar)

🌋 ELEMENTALIST

Core Mechanic: 4 Elemental Attunements
Vibe: Spell Caster, Stance Dancer, Elemental Wizard
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You rain down meteors in Fire, swap to Air to shock and stun foes, jump into Water to heal your group, and drop into Earth to turn your skin to stone, all in ten seconds!"

How It Plays

Elementalists can't swap weapons in combat, but they don't need to. You switch between 4 Attunements (Fire, Water, Air, Earth) on the fly, giving you 20 different weapon skills on a single set.

Specialization Options

  • Tempest: Overload your attunements to create elemental whirlwinds and tornadoes around yourself while buffing your team.
  • Weaver: Hold two elements at once (like Fire in one hand, Air in the other) to weave unique dual-element attacks with a Sword.
  • Catalyst: Summon an elemental Jade Sphere to place combo fields while swinging a heavy Hammer.
  • Evoker: Partner with spirit animal familiars (Fox, Otter, Hare, Toad) to charge up empowered elemental attacks.
👉 Pick Elementalist if you want: Flashy magic spells, lots of skills, and high fast-paced action.
Light Armor (Scholar)

🔮 MESMER

Core Mechanic: Illusions (Clones & Phantasms) & Shatters
Vibe: Trickster Duelist, Illusionist, Time & Space Master
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You strike an enemy and instantly create three clone copies of yourself. While the boss swings at your illusions, you explode them into purple glass shards for huge damage, then open a portal to warp your whole team across the room."

How It Plays

Mesmers use mind magic, clones, and portals. You spawn Clones to confuse enemies and draw attention away from yourself, then explode those copies on command using Shatter skills for big burst damage.

Specialization Options

  • Chronomancer: Control time! Open a time rift with Continuum Split, throw all your skills out, and snap back in time with full health and reset cooldowns.
  • Mirage: A fast desert duelist using Axes. You dodge while casting uninterrupted and trigger quick ambush attacks.
  • Virtuoso: Float psionic mental blades over your head, firing psychic daggers from range with a Dagger.
  • Troubadour: Replace clones with musical notes and phantasms with lingering instruments, buffing allies with ethereal music.
👉 Pick Mesmer if you want: Clone illusions, portal utility, time tricks, and purple magic effects.
Light Armor (Scholar)

💀 NECROMANCER

Core Mechanic: Life Force & Death Shroud
Vibe: Dark Sorcerer, Minion Commander, Tough Tank
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"Enemies surround you, but as they fall, you harvest their Life Force. You pop Death Shroud, turning into a shadowy reaper with a fresh second health bar, chilling enemies and stealing their health for yourself."

How It Plays

Necromancers are dark magic users who are tough to take down. Gathering Life Force from fallen foes lets you enter Death Shroud, giving you a full second health bar and a new set of attacks.

Specialization Options

  • Reaper: A heavy melee fighter. Enter Reaper's Shroud with a dark scythe and swing a big Greatsword through crowds.
  • Scourge: Trade your shroud for desert Sand Shades, shielding allies with barriers while putting dark conditions on enemies.
  • Harbinger: A dark alchemist with a Pistol. Drink volatile potions to gain huge speed and damage at the cost of some max health.
  • Ritualist: Enter Ritualist's Shroud to summon ancestral spirits and cast weapon spells that haunt enemies.
👉 Pick Necromancer if you want: Double health bars, undead minion pets, and strong solo survival.
