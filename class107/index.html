Heavy Armor (Soldier)

🛡️ GUARDIAN

Core Mechanics: Virtues (Justice, Resolve, Courage)
Playstyle Vibe: Holy Paladin, Tactician, Unyielding Wall, Fiery Smiter
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You stand in the center of a chaotic skirmish. An incoming wave of fiery arrows threatens your squad—you slam down a glowing spirit wall, deflecting every projectile into dust. Channeling your conviction, you leap headfirst into a horde of foes, bursting into golden flames and burning everything in reach while a shimmering shield continuously heals your allies."

Core Profession Fantasy

Guardians are devoted combatants who manifest divine-like Virtues. Even with lower natural health, you are virtually immortal when played well, compensating with constant health regeneration, reactive blocks (Aegis), and protective symbols painted on the ground.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Dragonhunter (Heart of Thorns): Wield a massive Longbow and lay deadly physical traps. You physicalize your virtues into tethering spears and wings of justice to snipe and shred boss defiance bars.
  • Firebrand (Path of Fire): Swap virtues for ancient, magical Tomes. You slice through enemies with a fiery Axe, chanting utility mantras and opening holy grimoires to scorch, heal, or shield entire squads.
  • Willbender (End of Dragons): A martial-arts assassin clad in heavy plate. You trade passive virtues for hyper-mobile dash skills, weaving dual-swords through the air and leaving trails of burning fire behind every footstep.
  • Luminary (Visions of Eternity): Enter the Radiant Forge! You wield weapons forged from pure light and sacred flame, adopting combat stances that burn enemies and shield allies with holy radiance.
👉 Pick Guardian if you want: High durability, glowing holy fire, and the ability to save your team from total wipe scenarios.
Heavy Armor (Soldier)

🌀 REVENANT

Core Mechanics: Legend Channeling & Energy
Playstyle Vibe: Mists Warrior, Conduit of History, Hybrid Spell-Knight
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"Blindfolded and pulsing with ethereal Mists magic, you swap your stance mid-fight. One second you are channeling the dark, demonic magic of Mallyx to siphon conditions from allies and dump them onto your foes—the next, you switch to the Legendary Assassin Shiro Tagachi, shadowstepping behind a dragon to deliver a lightning-fast flurry of sword strikes!"

Core Profession Fantasy

Revenants pull power directly from the Mists, reaching into history to channel legendary figures from Tyria's past. Instead of standard skill cooldowns, your abilities cost Energy, which resets every time you swap between your two equipped Legends.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Herald (Heart of Thorns): Channel the benevolent dragon Glint. You activate pulsing "Facets" that grant perma-boons (Might, Swiftness, Fury) to your entire raid group while wielding a sturdy off-hand Shield.
  • Renegade (Path of Fire): Channel the Charr rebel Kalla Scorchrazor. You fire razor-sharp shortbow arrows through Mists portals while summoning ghost-like warband members onto the field to rain down artillery.
  • Vindicator (End of Dragons): Channel two rival heroes simultaneously (Saint Viktor & Archemorus). You wield a heavy Greatsword and replace your normal dodge roll with a massive, high-leaping death-from-above strike.
  • Conduit (Visions of Eternity): Channel Razah, a cosmic being born of the Mists. You harness Cosmic Wisdom to deepen the bond between your legends, unleashing cosmic Resonance and unlocking new forms.
👉 Pick Revenant if you want: Dynamic energy management, shifting playstyles mid-fight, and channeling iconic lore figures.
Heavy Armor (Soldier)

⚔️ WARRIOR

Core Mechanics: Adrenaline & Weapon Mastery
Playstyle Vibe: Master of Arms, Berserker, Unstoppable Force
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You carve through a frontline with sweeping Greatsword strikes, building your Adrenaline with every swing. As your rage hits its boiling point, you unleash a crushing Burst attack that shatters the enemy’s stance, knock them to the ground, and rally your squad with an echoing war cry!"

Core Profession Fantasy

Warriors are the ultimate martial purists. Boasting the highest native health pool and heavy armor, they have access to the largest weapon arsenal in the game. They build Adrenaline by hitting enemies, then spend it on devastating, weapon-specific Burst Skills.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Berserker (Heart of Thorns): Succumb to pure rage and enter Berserk Mode. You swing off-hand Torches and unleash rapid-fire "Primal Bursts" that smash, burn, and crush enemies in seconds.
  • Spellbreaker (Path of Fire): Dual-wield Daggers to slice away magic itself. You utilize Full Counter to absorb incoming attacks, strip boons off enemies, and turn their own spellcasting power against them.
  • Bladesworn (End of Dragons): Technology meets the blade. You store combat "Flow" to draw a massive Gunsaber, pausing for a split second before unleashing a screen-clearing Dragon Slash that obliterates boss health bars.
  • Paragon (Visions of Eternity): Spear and shield in hand, you build Motivation to unleash vocal Chants and Refrains, inspiring your allies with barrier shields and rallying commands.
👉 Pick Warrior if you want: Raw physical power, massive weapon variety, and visceral, hard-hitting impact.
Medium Armor (Adventurer)

⚙️ ENGINEER

Core Mechanics: Tool Belt & Engineering Kits
Playstyle Vibe: Tech Genius, Mad Scientist, Gadgeteering Specialist
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You pull out a Flamethrower kit to incinerate a swarm of enemies, swap in a split second to throw a bundle of grenades over a wall, lay down an automated healing turret, and launch yourself across the battlefield with Rocket Boots—all without waiting for a single weapon swap cooldown."

Core Profession Fantasy

Engineers don't rely on magic—they rely on ingenuity. Instead of traditional weapon swapping, Engineers carry Kits (Grenades, Bombs, Flamethrowers, Elixirs) and a Tool Belt that gives every utility skill an extra activated ability on your F1–F5 keys.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Scrapper (Heart of Thorns): A hammer-wielding bruiser that builds hovering Gyros. You generate mobile wells of protection, supercharge speed for allies, and use a Function Gyro to revive downed teammates from afar.
  • Holosmith (Path of Fire): Harness solidified sunlight. You activate the Photon Forge to wield weapons made of hard-light, cauterizing foes with extreme heat—just don't overheat, or you'll explode!
  • Mechanist (End of Dragons): Build and command your own giant battle-bot, the Jade Mech CJ-1. Your mech tanks, deals massive damage, and fires lasers while you command it with a supportive Mace and Signets.
  • Amalgam (Visions of Eternity): Form a symbiotic bond with a mercurial ooze mold. You use Morph skills to alter your physical state, building up strain to trigger powerful Evolve shapeshifting bursts.
👉 Pick Engineer if you want: Endlessly versatile toolkits, futuristic technology, and high-apm (actions-per-minute) gameplay.
Medium Armor (Adventurer)

🏹 RANGER

Core Mechanics: Companion Pets & Wilderness Survival
Playstyle Vibe: Master Marksman, Beastmaster, Nature's Warden
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"From 1,500 range away, you unleash a volley of arrows from your Longbow while your loyal Smilodon lunges at an enemy's throat. When an assassin tries to ambush you in melee, you swap to a Greatsword, execute a fluid counter-evasion strike, and call upon the spirits of nature to heal your party."

Core Profession Fantasy

Rangers are masters of the wild who fight in perfect harmony with their Pets. You tame wild beasts across Tyria, swapping between two pets in combat to deal damage, inflict crowd control, or protect your allies.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Druid (Heart of Thorns): Commune with the stars and transform into a Celestial Avatar. Armed with a Staff and magical Glyphs, Druids are Tyria's premier burst healers and offensive support enablers.
  • Soulbeast (Path of Fire): Merge spirit and body with your pet by entering Beastmode. You absorb your pet's attributes, gaining their unique skills while striking from the shadows with main-hand Daggers.
  • Untamed (End of Dragons): Harness primal Echovald magic to juggle raw power back and forth between yourself and your pet. Unleash yourself for high Hammer damage, or unleash your pet for extreme defensive control.
  • Galeshot (Visions of Eternity): Master Tengu marksmanship with the Cyclone Bow. You manipulate wind and squall gusts to launch a flurry of chilled or whirlwind-infused arrows that pin enemies down.
👉 Pick Ranger if you want: A loyal animal companion, high-range archery, or powerful green-magic healing.
Medium Armor (Adventurer)

🗡️ THIEF

Core Mechanics: Initiative & Steal
Playstyle Vibe: Shadow Assassin, Acrobat, Lethal Sniper
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You shadowstep instantly through the Mists, appearing directly behind an enemy commander. You shadow-meld into total stealth, line up a lethal strike, and step back out before they even realize where the hit came from. Low on health? You steal a magical artifact directly from your target to turn the fight in your favor!"

Core Profession Fantasy

Thieves throw out traditional cooldowns in favor of Initiative—a fast-recharging resource that lets you spam your favorite weapon skills back-to-back. You use Steal to shadowstep to targets and create unique stolen weapons, relying on stealth, dodges, and shadowsteps to stay alive.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Daredevil (Heart of Thorns): An acrobatic martial artist with 3 Endurance bars. You vault over enemies with an upgraded dodge roll, beating foes down with a melee Staff and physical punches.
  • Deadeye (Path of Fire): A deadly sniper equipped with a Rifle. You drop into a kneeling position, mark your target for death, build up Malice, and consume it for a single, massive Stealth Snipe.
  • Specter (Path of Fire/EoD): A shadow-mage using Scepters and Wells. You shadowstep to allies, tethering your shadow to theirs to grant massive barrier shields, before entering Shadow Shroud to corrupt foes.
  • Antiquary (Visions of Eternity): Partner up with a Skritt assistant! You use Skritt Swipe to plunder rare artifacts and gamble with risky Double Edge skills that can be cast while on cooldown.
👉 Pick Thief if you want: Instant shadow-teleports, stealth attacks, and hyper-agile resource management.
Light Armor (Scholar)

🌋 ELEMENTALIST

Core Mechanics: 4 Elemental Attunements
Playstyle Vibe: High-APM Wizard, Stance-Dancer, Elemental Avatar
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You rain down meteors in Fire Attunement, instantly swap to Air to shock and stun your foes, drop into Water to surge a massive healing wave over your party, and sink into Earth to harden your skin into solid granite—all within a single 10-second rotation!"

Core Profession Fantasy

Elementalists can't swap weapons in combat, but they don't need to—they carry 4 Attunements (Fire, Water, Air, Earth) at all times, giving them access to 20 different weapon skills on a single set! You are a glass cannon with immense area-of-effect damage, high mobility, and incredible team support.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Tempest (Heart of Thorns): Channel elemental shouts and Overload your attunements. You create swirling elemental tornadoes and firestorms around yourself, imbuing allies with powerful elemental Auras.
  • Weaver (Path of Fire): Master dual-attunement weaving. You hold Fire in your main hand and Air in your off-hand, creating unique Dual Attacks in your 3-slot that let you fluidly dance through melee combat with a Sword.
  • Catalyst (End of Dragons): Summon an elemental Jade Sphere to create pulsing combo fields. You wield a heavy Hammer, spinning elemental orbs around yourself to stack massive stat buffs before launching them at foes.
  • Evoker (Visions of Eternity): Partner with spiritual animal companions (Fox, Otter, Hare, Toad). You meditate on a chosen element to reduce its recharge, charging up your familiar's basic and empowered abilities.
👉 Pick Elementalist if you want: Flashy magic spells, constant key-pressing, and master-level skill ceilings.
Light Armor (Scholar)

🔮 MESMER

Core Mechanics: Illusions (Clones & Phantasms) & Shatters
Playstyle Vibe: Trickster, Illusionist Duelist, Master of Time & Space
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"You strike an enemy, instantly summoning three identical illusionary Clones of yourself. As the confused boss swings wildly at your copies, you detonate all three with a Mind Wrack Shatter—shattering the illusions into purple glass shards that explode for massive burst damage while you open a spatial Portal to teleport your entire squad across the arena!"

Core Profession Fantasy

Mesmers are duelists who manipulate mind magic, space, and time. You create Clones (which confuse enemies and draw aggro) and Phantasms (which perform powerful attacks), then explode them on command using Shatter skills.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Chronomancer (Heart of Thorns): Control time itself! You manipulate Alacrity and Quickness, using Continuum Split to open a time rift, cast all your powerful spells, and jump back in time to regain full health and reset all cooldowns.
  • Mirage (Path of Fire): An elusive desert duelist using Axes. You replace your dodge roll with Mirage Cloak, allowing you to dodge while casting spells uninterrupted and triggering deadly Ambush Attacks.
  • Virtuoso (End of Dragons): Ditch the clones and manifest floating psionic Blades above your head. You wield a Dagger to fire mental blades like pink psychic daggers from range.
  • Troubadour (Visions of Eternity): Replace clones with musical Notes and shatters with lingering Phantasmal Instruments. You recite Tyrian tales to empower your allies with an ethereal orchestra.
👉 Pick Mesmer if you want: Mind games, time manipulation, team portals, and striking purple psychic aesthetics.
Light Armor (Scholar)

💀 NECROMANCER

Core Mechanics: Life Force & Death Shroud
Playstyle Vibe: Dark Sorcerer, Undead Commander, Unkillable Juggernaut
🔥 Picture Yourself in Battle:

"Enemies surround you, but you harvest their dying Life Force to fill your shadow gauge. You activate Death Shroud—transforming into a terrifying reaper of death with a brand-new health bar. You chill foes to the bone, corrupt their positive boons into crippling conditions, and siphon their life energy straight into your own blood stream!"

Core Profession Fantasy

Necromancers are masters of the dark arts who refuse to die. By gathering Life Force from deaths and attacks, you use Death Shroud as a massive second health pool and secondary weapon bar. You command undead minions, inflict debilitating conditions, and corrupt enemy buffs.

Elite Specialization Pathways

  • Reaper (Heart of Thorns): A terrifying melee juggernaut. You enter Reaper's Shroud with a ghostly dark scythe, screaming chilling war cries and cleaving through hordes with a heavy Greatsword.
  • Scourge (Path of Fire): Trade your shroud for desert Sand Shades. You summon pulsing shades on the field to grant massive Barrier shields to allies while drowning enemies in torment and fear.
  • Harbinger (End of Dragons): A dark alchemist gunslinger. You fire corrosive shots from a Pistol and drink volatile Elixirs, afflicting yourself with Blight (reducing your max HP) in exchange for absurd damage and attack speed.
  • Ritualist (Visions of Eternity): Enter Ritualist's Shroud to summon ancestral spirits (Anguish, Wanderlust, Preservation) and cast weapon spells to haunt foes and buff allies.
👉 Pick Necromancer if you want: Massive health pools, dark/gothic magic, easy solo gameplay, and high survivability.
