Field Archive // 102

Exploration

Open the map, pick a direction, and see what happens. Tyria rewards curiosity more than following a perfect route.

Maps Events Hero Points
At a Glance

What you're looking at

Waypoints

Your fast-travel network

Unlock waypoints as you pass them. Once they're open, you can use them to quickly return to places you've already visited.

Events

Follow what's happening

Orange events appear while you're exploring. Jump in when one looks interesting. You don't need to be in a party to help.

Map Completion

Optional, not homework

Hearts, vistas, points of interest, waypoints, and hero challenges make up the core map-completion objectives.

Core Lesson

Don't turn Tyria into a checklist

Open the world map with M and you'll see plenty of things you could chase. You don't need to clear every icon before moving on. Use them as landmarks and reasons to explore.

If an event starts nearby, follow it. If you see a vista on a cliff, figure out how to reach it. If another player runs toward something interesting, see what they're doing. Some of the best parts of Guild Wars 2 happen because you got distracted.

01 // Map Icons

Learn the handful that matter

You don't need to memorize the whole map legend. Start with the icons you'll see constantly.

Waypoint

Unlock it once and you can fast travel back later.

Point of Interest

A location worth discovering. Usually you just need to reach it.

Vista

Reach the marked spot and interact with it for a view of the area. Getting there is sometimes the real challenge.

02 // Renown Hearts

Help out around the area

Renown Hearts give you a few different ways to help an NPC or local area. You usually don't have to do one exact task over and over.

Once a heart is finished, that NPC becomes a karma merchant. Some sell useful gear, gathering tools, ingredients, or other local items.

Good Habit

Don't tunnel-vision on the heart bar. If an event starts nearby, go help with the event and come back later if you still need the heart.

03 // Dynamic Events

Follow the orange markers

Events are things happening in the world right now: defending a village, escorting someone, defeating a boss, collecting supplies, or dealing with whatever trouble just showed up.

You can usually join simply by showing up and helping. Some events also lead directly into another event, so stick around for a moment after one ends.

Simple Rule

See a bunch of players suddenly running in the same direction? There's probably a reason.

04 // Hero Challenges

Points for your profession

Hero Challenges award Hero Points that you can spend on profession skills and specializations.

You'll also earn enough Hero Points just by leveling to 80 to unlock your profession's core skills and specializations. Map challenges simply let you unlock things sooner and give you more points to use later.

Don't Stress

Missing a Hero Challenge while leveling won't ruin your character. You can always come back.

Map Completion

What does “complete the map” actually mean?

When players talk about map completion, they mean finding all of the required exploration objectives on that map.

Completing maps is useful and can be rewarding, but you don't need 100% completion to keep progressing through the game.

Veteran Field Note

Grab waypoints when you're nearby

You don't need to clear every map, but unlocking waypoints while you're already passing through saves a lot of running later.

Future-you will appreciate having a nearby waypoint when a friend asks for help, an event pops up, or you suddenly need to return to that corner of the map.

Try It in Tyria

Take a walk

Pick a map you're already on and spend a few minutes exploring without worrying about clearing it.

Unlock

Grab a couple of nearby waypoints so you have easy places to return to later.

Wander

Pick a vista, point of interest, or Hero Challenge and figure out how to reach it.

Get Distracted

If an event appears on the way, forget your original plan for a minute and go see what's happening.
