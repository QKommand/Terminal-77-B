Lesson Goal

[LESSON 2 OF 6]

Once you learn to how to hold your weapon in the barracks, it's time to teach you how to read the battle, work with local allies, and prepare for Level 80 endgame content.

Module 01

Map Literacy & World Exploration

Open your World Map (M Key) to check regional goals. Unlock blue diamond Waypoints for fast travel during map events. Complete Renown Hearts to buy local gear with Karma, and climb terrain to reach Vistas and Points of Interest.

M Key = World Map | Blue Diamonds = Waypoints
Module 02

Hero Point Logistics

Gather Hero Points to unlock your skill trees and build trait lines. You earn points automatically as you level up from 1 to 80, but communing with Hero Challenges on maps speeds up unlocking your first 3 Core Trait lines early.

Levels 1–80 + Map Hero Challenges
Module 03

Core Dungeons & Group Tactics

Dungeons introduce 5-player team combat starting at Level 30. Run Story Mode for story context, then try Explorable Mode at Level 35 to practice staying grouped, dodging boss attacks, and reviving downed teammates.

Level 30 Story | Level 35 Explorable
Module 04

Pact Commander Initiation

When you hit Level 80, unlock your Central Tyria Mastery Tracks. Set Pact Commander as your active track right away and train it to Tier 5 (Advanced Logistics) to get passive Auto-Loot across all maps.

Goal: Pact Commander Tier 5 Auto-Loot

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 4 DONE]
  • Unlock every blue diamond Waypoint you see on a new map for easy travel.
  • Complete Hero Challenges on starter maps to finish 3 core trait lines.
  • Finish your first 5-player dungeon Story Mode at Level 30 (Ascalonian Catacombs).
  • Set Pact Commander as your active mastery line at Level 80 to start training Auto-Loot.
