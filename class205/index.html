Lesson Goal

[ADVANCED TRACK]

Your armor determines your base protection, while your stat prefix tunes the build for raw power, sharp criticals, or heavy force with ferocity.

Module 01

Armor Weight Classes

Every character class wears one specific armor weight: Heavy, Medium, or Light. This acts as your physical shield against incoming damage.

Heavy | Medium | Light
Module 02

Gear Slots & Defense

Equip 6 armor pieces for full physical protection. Note that physical defense ratings do not protect against nasty conditions like burning or bleeding.

6 Slots | Exotic/Ascended Max
Module 03

Attribute Focus Scale

Use the simple 1–4 scale to see which stats matter most on your gear, ranging from minor support up to your main combat focus.

Scale: 1 (Off-Stat) to 4 (Primary)
Module 04

Fashion & Dyes

Stats and visual looks are completely separate. You can change your visual style anytime in the Wardrobe without changing your combat power.

Wardrobe Skins | Infinite Dyes

Practice Drill Checklist

  • Check Your Weight Class: Open the Hero Panel (H) and confirm your character is wearing the correct armor class.
