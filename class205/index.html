Lesson Goal

[ADVANCED TRACK]

Think of your armor like a sports car. Your armor weight class is the chassis that absorbs physical crashes, while your stat prefix tunes the engine for raw speed, sharp handling, or heavy pulling power.

Module 01

Armor Weight Classes

Every profession is locked to a specific armor weight class (Heavy, Medium, or Light) which determines your baseline physical damage absorption.

Heavy | Medium | Light
Module 02

Gear Slots & Defense

Equip 6 armor pieces for maximum defense against strikes. Note that physical defense ratings do not mitigate condition damage like burning or bleeding.

6 Slots | Exotic/Ascended Max
Module 03

Attribute Focus Scale

Use the 1–4 scale to measure stat weightings across prefixes, from minor support (Rank 2) to maximum primary focus (Rank 4).

Scale: 1 (Off-Stat) to 4 (Primary)
Module 04

Fashion & Dyes

Combat stats and visual looks are completely separated. Unlock skins via the Wardrobe and customize colors across 4 dye channels for free.

Wardrobe Skins | Infinite Dyes
