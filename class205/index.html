Armor & Attributes

Gear isn't just a bigger number. Your equipment helps decide what your character is good at, from direct damage and conditions to survival, healing, and boon support.

Armor Attributes Stat Sets
At a Glance

What your gear is doing

Armor

Gives defense and stats

Your six armor pieces contribute Defense and attributes to your character.

Attributes

Shape your strengths

Power, Precision, Condition Damage, Healing Power, and other attributes change how your character performs.

Stat Sets

Package stats together

Named combinations such as Berserker's or Marauder package several attributes together for a particular purpose.

Core Lesson

Gear should support what you're trying to do

Think of your gear like choosing tools for a job. A hammer isn't better than a wrench because it's heavier. It depends on what you're trying to accomplish.

Guild Wars 2 works the same way. A character focused on direct strikes wants different attributes from someone focused on damaging conditions, healing allies, or maintaining boons.

Before copying a build, start with a simpler question: what job is this gear helping me do?

Part 1 // Armor

Your profession determines your armor weight

Every profession belongs to one armor class: Heavy, Medium, or Light. You don't choose between the three. Your profession determines which type of armor it can equip.

Important Distinction

Heavy armor doesn't automatically make a build tanky

Armor weight affects your Defense, but survival comes from more than the armor icon next to your profession.

Your health, attributes, traits, defensive skills, dodging, positioning, boons, healing, and encounter knowledge all matter too.

A Light Armor profession isn't automatically fragile, and a Heavy Armor profession isn't automatically a tank.

Part 2 // Attributes

Learn what the common stats are trying to do

You don't need to memorize every possible stat combination yet. Start by recognizing the job of the major attributes.

Power

Increases the strength of your direct strike damage.

Precision

Increases your chance to land critical hits.

Ferocity

Increases the damage dealt by your critical hits.

Condition Damage

Increases damage from damaging conditions such as Burning and Bleeding.

Expertise

Increases Condition Duration.

Vitality

Increases your maximum health.

Toughness

Increases Armor and reduces incoming strike damage.

Healing Power

Improves the effectiveness of many healing abilities.

Concentration

Increases Boon Duration.

Part 4 // Stat Combinations

What does “Berserker's” actually mean?

Gear often comes with a named stat combination. Players commonly call these stat sets or prefixes.

The name is shorthand for a package of attributes. Once you learn a few common names, build guides become much easier to read.

Berserker's

Power

Power + Precision + Ferocity

A straightforward direct-damage combination.

Marauder

Power + Health

Power + Precision + Vitality + Ferocity

Offensive stats with additional health.

Viper's

Condition

Power + Condition Damage + Precision + Expertise

A damage-focused combination built around conditions while still supporting strike damage.

Celestial

Hybrid

Provides a broad mixture of attributes.

Useful for builds that benefit from several different kinds of stats instead of specializing heavily in one direction.

Minstrel's

Support

Toughness + Healing Power + Vitality + Concentration

A defensive support combination built around healing, boon duration, and survival.

Assassin's

Critical

Precision + Power + Ferocity

Similar offensive attributes to Berserker's, with greater emphasis on Precision.

Common Gear Trap

More different stats doesn't always mean a stronger character

New players often equip whatever item has the biggest number or highest rarity and slowly end up with several unrelated attributes.

None of those individual pieces may be bad. The problem is that the whole set may be pulling your character in several directions at once.

Start thinking about a purpose. Direct damage? Conditions? Support? More survival? Your gear should gradually begin telling the same story.

Part 5 // Gear Quality

Rarity and stats are different questions

Gear quality affects the strength of the equipment, but higher rarity doesn't automatically mean the stat combination is right for your build.

Exotic

Strong starting point

Exotic equipment is a practical place to begin putting together a complete level-80 build.

Ascended

Higher equipment tier

Ascended equipment provides stronger attributes than Exotic gear and becomes important for some endgame progression.

Legendary

Maximum flexibility

Legendary equipment shares the same attribute strength as Ascended equipment while providing major convenience and build flexibility.

New Level-80 Players

You don't need Legendary gear to start playing seriously

Don't turn gearing into a mountain before you've even started climbing.

A sensible set of Exotic equipment with attributes that actually fit what you're trying to do is enough to begin learning many level-80 activities.

Better gear can come later. Understanding why you're wearing something matters first.

Part 6 // Wardrobe

Your stats don't decide your fashion

Guild Wars 2 separates the appearance of your armor from the equipment's combat attributes.

Once you've unlocked an armor skin, the Wardrobe lets you customize how your equipment looks without needing to wear that original item for its stats.

Dyes let you customize the available color channels on your equipped armor skins.

Translation

Build for combat. Dress for yourself.

Veteran Field Note

Don't copy the gear before you understand the job

Build guides are useful, but a list of equipment isn't the same thing as understanding a build.

When you see a recommended stat set, ask why it's there. Is the build chasing critical hits? Condition duration? Boon duration? Healing? Extra health?

Once you can answer that, you're starting to read builds instead of simply copying them.

Try It in Tyria

Read your own equipment

Open your Hero Panel and inspect the equipment you're currently wearing.

Check

Look at all six armor slots and identify the stat combinations you're currently using.

Ask

What are most of those attributes trying to improve: direct damage, conditions, support, or survival?

Explore

Open the Wardrobe and Dye sections so you know where appearance customization lives.
