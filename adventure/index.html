/// TACTICAL ENEMY LIBRARY & COMBAT REFLEXES

Universal Battle Mechanics & Reaction Field Manual

MECHANIC 01 BLUE BAR

Defiance Bars (Breakbars)

When a blue bar appears under a boss health bar, strike with Hard CC (Stun, Daze, Knockback) or Soft CC (Chilled, Slow, Cripple) to interrupt devastating attacks and multiply incoming damage by +50%.

MECHANIC 02 RED RINGS

Telegraphs & Evade Windows

Orange circles and red attack cones indicate high-damage zones. Time your single dodge key right as the orange ring fills to gain complete invulnerability frames (i-frames) through heavy hits.

MECHANIC 03 STACKING

SQUAD POSITIONING & BOONS

Stand close behind or beside the enemy alongside your squad commander. Boons (Might, Quickness, Alacrity) and healing fields have a tight 240–360 unit radius; staying stacked keeps party DPS maxed.
