Currencies & the Account Wallet

Tyria has a lot of currencies. You don't need to memorize them all. Learn where they live, what activity they belong to, and when they're worth spending.

Wallet Currencies Spending
At a Glance

The important part

Earn

Activities pay differently

Open-world maps, Fractals, PvP, WvW, raids, achievements, and other systems often reward their own currencies.

Store

The Wallet remembers

Supported currencies automatically live in your account Wallet instead of filling your bags.

Spend

Spend toward a goal

Before emptying a currency at the first vendor you find, check what else that currency can buy.

Core Lesson

Don't treat every currency like gold

Gold is flexible. Many other currencies are more like tickets for a particular part of the game.

Fractal currency tells you that you've been playing Fractals. A map currency usually connects back to that region. WvW currencies come from WvW and help buy WvW-related rewards.

Once you think of currencies as progress markers for activities, the Wallet gets a lot less intimidating.

Part 1 // The Wallet

One account, one shared currency ledger

Open your Inventory and select the Wallet to see the currencies stored on your account.

When one character earns a Wallet currency, another character on the same account can generally access that same balance.

Automatic

No manual deposit

Wallet currencies are deposited automatically when you receive them.

Shared

Account-wide balance

You don't need to move most Wallet currencies between your characters.

Convenient

Keeps bags cleaner

Many tokens and keys that once could have been inventory clutter are tracked directly in the Wallet.

Important Difference

Wallet currency isn't the same as Material Storage

Coins, Karma, Astral Acclaim, Fractal Relics, and similar currencies belong in the Wallet.

Ore, wood, cloth, leather, trophies, and other crafting ingredients usually belong in Material Storage instead.

If something is still sitting in your bags, don't assume the Wallet is broken. It may be an item, crafting material, container, or token that uses normal inventory storage.

Part 2 // Common Currencies

A few names you'll see everywhere

You don't need every Wallet entry memorized. These are useful ones to recognize because they show up across a lot of account progression.

Gold, Silver & Copper

Your general-purpose money for vendors, the Trading Post, waypoints, crafting costs, and many other parts of the game.

Karma

Earned from many activities and spent with Karma vendors throughout the world.

Astral Acclaim

Earned through Wizard's Vault objectives and spent on rewards inside the Vault.

Laurels

An account reward currency used with Laurel merchants and for a variety of account progression purchases.

Spirit Shards

A level-80 progression currency used heavily in Mystic Forge and advanced crafting systems.

Research Notes

Obtained through the Research system and used by several endgame vendors and crafting recipes.

Worth Knowing // Astral Acclaim

Don't let Wizard's Vault rewards sit forgotten

Wizard's Vault objectives award Astral Acclaim, which you spend directly inside the Vault.

The available rewards and purchase limits make it worth checking the Vault instead of simply letting your balance pile up without a plan.

You don't need to optimize every reward. Just remember that Astral Acclaim is meant to be spent.

Part 3 // Activity Currencies

The activity usually explains the currency

Harder or more specialized activities often have currencies tied to their own reward systems.

Fractals

Fractal Relics

Used within the Fractal reward and progression ecosystem.

WvW

WvW currencies

World versus World has its own currencies for equipment, progression, and WvW-related rewards.

PvP

PvP currencies

Structured PvP has currencies connected to ranked play and its reward systems.

Raids

Raid currencies

Raid encounters award currencies used for raid equipment and other long-term rewards.

Part 4 // Regional Currencies

Every map doesn't need the same money

Expansions and Living World regions introduce their own currencies, tokens, keys, and materials.

These commonly buy rewards connected to that region: equipment, skins, recipes, collections, keys, or progression items.

Don't Memorize the Names

If you suddenly start earning a currency you've never seen before, ask a simpler question: “What map or activity am I getting this from?”

That usually tells you where to start looking for the vendor or reward system that uses it.

Part 5 // Keys & Special Wallet Entries

Some “currencies” don't look like money

The Wallet also tracks several map keys and other resources used to open regional reward containers or interact with particular systems.

That means you may earn something that sounds like a physical key without ever seeing it appear inside your bags.

Inventory Tip

Before wondering where a map key disappeared to, check the Wallet.

Not Everything Is Account-Wide

A few resources belong somewhere else

The Wallet is account-wide, but Guild Wars 2 still has resources that belong to a character, guild, inventory, or other system.

Character Resources

Hero Points belong to the character earning them. WvW Supply is also carried by the character currently using it.

Guild Resources

Guild systems have their own resources for upgrades and guild progression rather than using your personal Wallet balance.

Fashion Currency

Transmutation Charges

Transmutation Charges are stored as currency and are used through the Wardrobe when changing the appearance of equipment.

This is another good example of why the Wallet isn't just a pile of “money.” Some entries power specific account systems rather than normal vendors.

Part 6 // Before You Spend

Unknown currency? Don't panic-spend it.

Some currencies are easy to replace. Others are attached to longer progression paths or expensive rewards.

1

Ask what you want

Are you gearing a character, buying a skin, working on a collection, or saving for something bigger?

2

Check what the currency buys

Look at the vendor, Wallet description, achievement, or wiki entry before spending a large amount.

3

Spend with a reason

You don't need to hoard everything forever. Just know what you're trading away and why.

Returning Player Reset

Your Wallet looks like alphabet soup?

Ignore most of it.

You probably accumulated years of currencies from maps, festivals, expansions, competitive modes, and systems you haven't touched in a long time.

Start with the content you're playing today. Learn the currencies that content gives you. The rest can stay safely in the Wallet until you need them.

Veteran Field Note

Don't farm a currency before you know why you need it

It's easy to hear that a map or activity is an efficient farm and start collecting thousands of something with no actual goal.

Pick the reward first. Then figure out what currency it needs.

Farming works better when the destination comes before the road.

Try It in Tyria

Read your Wallet instead of memorizing it

Open the Wallet and pick three currencies you already have.

Identify

Pick one currency you recognize and explain where you normally earn it.

Investigate

Pick one currency you don't recognize and find out what activity or region it belongs to.

Plan

Find one currency you're currently saving and identify what you actually want to buy with it.

Lesson Complete

You don't need to know every currency

You just need to know how to figure one out when it becomes relevant.

The Wallet stores the history of what you've played. Let your current goal decide which part of that history matters today.
