Lesson Goal

[EXPERT TRACK]

Think of your account wallet as a multi-currency global bank account. Instead of clogging your inventory slots, earned currencies automatically deposit into a shared account ledger that every character on your account can spend from instantly.

Module 01

Core Economy and Universal Currencies

Gold, Silver, Copper

Primary Tyrian currency used on the Trading Post and with vendor NPCs.

Gems

Premium store currency, convertible to and from gold through the Currency Exchange.

Karma

Non-tradeable points earned through open world events and spent at vendors world-wide.

Astral Acclaim

Earned through Wizard's Vault tasks to buy high-value seasonal rewards.

Spirit Shards

Earned via excess experience at level 80 to craft elite weapons and Mystic Forge recipes.

Laurels

Account reward currency used for specialized gear and crafting materials.

Transmutation Charges

Currency spent in the Wardrobe to alter gear skins.

Research Notes

Acquired by salvaging crafted items using Research Kits.

Module 04

Wallet Keys and Storage Exceptions

Key Storage

Specialized keys like Pact Crowbars, Exalted Keys, Trader Keys, and Jade Keycards store directly in the wallet rather than taking up inventory space.

Character Bound Exceptions

Hero Points (for trait unlocks) and WvW Supply (for siege building) stay bound to individual characters instead of the global account wallet.

Guild Funds

Favor (earned from Guild Missions) and Aetherium (generated by Guild Mines) fuel guild hall upgrades.
