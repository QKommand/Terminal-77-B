Astral Acclaim & the Wizard's Vault

Complete objectives, earn Astral Acclaim, then choose the rewards that actually matter to you.

Objectives Astral Acclaim Rewards
At a Glance

How the Vault works

Complete

Do objectives

Daily, weekly, special, and event objectives award Astral Acclaim.

Claim

Collect your rewards

Finished objectives still need to be claimed through the Vault before their Astral Acclaim enters your Wallet.

Spend

Pick your reward

Instead of following one fixed reward track, spend Astral Acclaim on the Vault rewards you want.

Core Lesson

The Vault is a reward shop you earn your way into

Think of the Wizard's Vault like being handed tokens for doing normal game activities.

The game gives you objectives, those objectives award Astral Acclaim, and you decide which available rewards are worth buying.

That last part matters. You don't need to buy everything. The Vault works better when you start with a goal instead of emptying your currency just because you have it.

Part 1 // Opening the Vault

Your objectives live in one place

Open the Wizard's Vault to see your current objectives, Astral Acclaim, available rewards, and seasonal progress.

Objectives

What can I do?

Check your daily, weekly, and special objectives and choose what fits your play session.

Astral Rewards

What can I buy?

Browse the current reward inventory before deciding what you're saving for.

Legacy Rewards

What did I miss?

Previous seasonal cosmetics can remain available through the Legacy Rewards section at a higher Astral Acclaim cost.

Part 2 // Objective Types

Different clocks, same currency

Wizard's Vault objectives come in a few different time scales. You don't need to clear every category to make the system useful.

Daily

Short objectives that reset each day and are meant to fit into regular play.

Weekly

Larger objectives with more time to complete them before the weekly reset.

Special

Longer-running objectives connected to seasonal content, releases, and other activities.

Events

Festivals and bonus events can add their own Astral Acclaim objectives.

Choose How You Play

Daily and weekly choices can be different

The Vault can pull objectives from PvE, PvP, WvW, or a mixture of those categories.

Your daily and weekly category selections can be configured separately. That means you might prefer simple PvE dailies while choosing a different mix for your weekly goals.

If you haven't completed an objective from that set yet, you can also change the allocation instead of being stuck with it until the next reset.

Don't Turn It Into Chores

The Vault should follow your play, not control it

It's easy to open a daily list and suddenly feel like the game handed you homework.

If you were already planning to run events, Fractals, PvP, or WvW, see whether the Vault can reward you for doing it.

You don't need to rearrange your entire evening around one objective you don't enjoy.

Part 3 // Astral Acclaim

Earn it, then remember to spend it

Astral Acclaim is the account-wide currency used inside the Wizard's Vault.

Shared

Account currency

Astral Acclaim belongs to your account rather than one specific character.

Soft Cap

1,300 Astral Acclaim

Once you're at or above the normal cap, you generally need to spend some before claiming more objective rewards.

Carries Over

Seasons don't wipe it

Unspent Astral Acclaim carries into the next Vault season.

Watch the Cap

Don't leave completed rewards trapped behind 1,300

The Vault uses a soft cap of 1,300 Astral Acclaim.

If your balance is full, spend some before continuing to claim completed objectives.

A good habit is simple: check your balance whenever you visit the reward shop.

Part 4 // Reward Types

What are you actually buying?

Vault inventory changes over time, but rewards generally fall into a few useful buckets.

Account Value

Currency, materials, account upgrades, and other rewards that support general progression.

Cosmetics

Skins and other appearance rewards for players who care more about fashion than raw progression.

Equipment

Gear-related rewards can help characters move toward stronger level-80 equipment.

Long-Term Projects

Some rewards can support Legendary equipment, crafting, or other larger account goals.

Part 5 // What Should I Buy?

Start with your account, not somebody else's shopping list

Players will absolutely make spreadsheets ranking Vault rewards by theoretical value.

Those can be useful later. First, figure out what your account actually needs.

1

Look for limited rewards

Check whether something you want has a seasonal purchase limit before spending everything elsewhere.

2

Buy toward a current goal

Building equipment? Crafting? Working toward a Legendary? Saving gold? Choose rewards that shorten that road.

3

Buy the cosmetic if you actually want it

Not every decision needs to be maximum gold efficiency. If the reward is the thing you want, that's a legitimate use of the currency.

Legacy Rewards

Missing a cosmetic season isn't the end of the world

Seasonal cosmetic rewards can move into the Legacy Rewards section after their original season ends.

Legacy versions cost more Astral Acclaim than they did during their featured season, so buying something while it's current can still save currency.

But you don't need to treat every seasonal cosmetic like a panic purchase.

Easy Thing to Forget

Completed doesn't always mean claimed

When an objective is finished, return to the Wizard's Vault and make sure its reward has actually been claimed.

Daily and weekly completion rewards are especially easy to leave sitting in the interface if you're focused on playing.

Returning Player Reset

Haven't used the Vault before?

Don't start by studying every reward.

1

Choose your objective categories

Pick PvE, PvP, WvW, or a mix based on what you actually play.

2

Complete normal activities

Let the Vault overlap with things you were already planning to do.

3

Browse before buying

Look through the reward shop once so you know what you're saving toward.

Veteran Field Note

Optimize after you understand what you're optimizing for

There are efficient Astral Acclaim purchases. Their value also changes when the Vault inventory changes.

That's why this guide isn't giving you a permanent “buy these five things first” list.

Learn what the rewards do, identify your current goal, then use current community guides if you want to optimize the last few percent.

Try It in Tyria

Use the Vault once with a purpose

No checklist. Just get familiar with the loop.

Look

Open the Wizard's Vault and read your current daily and weekly objectives.

Browse

Look through Astral Rewards and find one item you would actually like to earn.

Connect

Find one objective that overlaps with something you already wanted to play today.

Lesson Complete

The Vault is a bonus layer, not your boss

Check your objectives, collect Astral Acclaim while playing, and spend it on something useful or something you simply like.

That's enough. You don't need to turn every reset into a second job.
