Build Theory: Personal Comfort Over Max Numbers

Crafting a build is like building a custom vehicle. Instead of copying what someone else drives, you want to pick stats that fit your actual driving style. A build with massive damage numbers is useless if you find yourself constantly dying or struggling to stay alive. By understanding what each stat does, you can mix and match gear until your character feels comfortable, fun, and capable.

The Attribute Guide: How Stats Build Your Playstyle

Understanding these core building blocks helps you craft any build in the game.

1. Direct Strike Damage (Power Builds)

If you like direct, instant damage with big numbers right when your sword hits:

  • Power (Base Engine): The muscle behind every physical swing and projectile.
  • Precision (Bullseye Chance): Raises your Critical Hit Chance toward 100%.
  • Ferocity (Turbo Crunch): Makes those critical hits deal much higher bonus damage.
2. Damage Over Time (Condition Builds)

If you prefer inflicting Bleeding, Burning, or Poison that drains enemies while you move around:

  • Condition Damage (Poison Punch): Increases the tick size of your damage-over-time effects.
  • Expertise (Timer Stretch): Increases Condition Duration, making those damage ticks last longer on targets.
3. Survival & Cushioning

If you want extra room for mistakes or need to tank heavy incoming attacks:

  • Vitality (Health Bar Padding): Expands your total health pool. It also increases your maximum temporary shield (Barrier) capacity!
  • Toughness (Armor Plating): Reduces incoming physical damage from enemy attacks.
4. Support & Buff Uptime

If you want your positive buffs to stay active longer or care about healing allies:

  • Concentration (Buff Keeper): Extends the duration of all positive boons (like Speed, Might, and Protection).
  • Healing Power (First Aid): Boosts the output of your active healing skills and regeneration effects.
