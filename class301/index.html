How Gear Works (In Simple Terms)

Think of building your character like setting up a car. Power is your engine size, Precision is how often you hit top speed, Ferocity makes those top speeds extra fast, and Vitality is your crash padding. You don't have to follow a rigid blueprint—you can swap armor pieces around to fit how safe or aggressive you want to play.

What The Stats Actually Do

A simple look at what each gear stat adds to your character.

1. Damage Stats (Raw Punch)

• Power: Flat muscle. Makes every sword swing hit harder.

• Precision: Critical hit chance. Gives you a higher chance to land lucky double-damage strikes.

• Ferocity: Makes those lucky critical hits do even bigger numbers.

2. Safety Stats (Health Padding)

• Vitality: Gives you a larger health bar.

• Why it helps Barrier: Temporary yellow shields (Barrier) cap at 50% of your max health. More health means bigger shield bubbles!

3. Utility Stats (Buff Keepers)

• Concentration: Makes positive buffs (like Speed, Might, and Shields) last longer on you and your friends so they don't wear off in combat.

How The Luminary Works

Two simple mechanics that make this build shine.

1. Keeping Up Resolution

Resolution is a golden buff that cuts incoming condition damage and boosts your damage output.

• Drops from Greatsword 4 (Symbol on the ground).
• Keeps your damage high automatically just by playing naturally.

2. Light Aura Explosions

• Get a Light Aura: Leap through light fields or turn on your stance skills.

• Pop the Aura: Tap stance skills to detonate your aura for a big area explosion.
