Build Theory: Style Over Big Numbers

In this lesson, we are looking at one of the elite specializations called the Luminary. We use the Luminary as a hands-on guide to show how build theory works in actual gameplay. A great build isn't about copying high damage charts—if a build hits hard but feels too fragile to survive, your real damage drops to zero. Your gear should be a conscious choice about your personal combat style, balancing aggressive power with the safety cushion you feel comfortable playing.

Attribute Breakdown: Tuning Your Gear

How individual stats shape your combat feel.

1. Raw Output (Power & Precision)

• Power (Engine Size): Flat muscle. Makes all your attacks hit harder.

• Precision (Bullseye Chance): Determines how often you land double-damage critical hits.

2. Extra Punch & Buffs (Ferocity & Concentration)

• Ferocity (Turbo Boost): Adds extra damage to your critical hits.

• Concentration (Buff Duration): Keeps your positive boons (like Speed, Might, and Resolution) active longer.

3. Safety Padding (Vitality)

• Vitality (Health Cushion): Adds max health to your health bar.

• Bigger Shields: Since yellow temporary shields (Barrier) cap at 50% of your max health, higher Vitality directly makes your shields larger!
