[BUILD EDITOR & ANALYSIS]

Before copying a list of armor pieces, you need to understand how attributes work together. Think of build tailoring like tuning a race car: Power is your engine size, Precision is your steering accuracy, Ferocity is your turbocharger, Concentration is your fuel tank capacity, and Vitality is your roll cage. Adjusting these dials shapes your character's combat profile.

Core Attribute Breakdown

How each stat directly influences your Power Luminary performance.

1. Raw Output (Power & Precision)

• Power (Base Engine): Determines your flat damage multiplier on all physical hits.

• Precision (Bullseye Chance): Raises Critical Hit Chance toward 100%. Luminary traits grant free crit chance when Resolution or Fury is active, allowing you to save stat points for other areas.

2. Impact & Utility (Ferocity & Concentration)

• Ferocity (Turbo Crunch): Increases critical hit damage numbers.

• Concentration (Boon Stretch): Extends the duration of Might, Resolution, and Quickness so your combat buffs never drop off during solo play.

3. Health Cushion (Vitality)

• Vitality (Roll Cage Padding): Adds flat max HP to your health orb.

• Barrier Synergy: Since Barrier scales off your maximum health pool (capped at 50% max HP), extra Vitality directly increases the size of your yellow shield layer!

The Engine Mechanics (Resolution & Light Aura)

The Power Luminary damage output is driven by two interlocking mechanical engines: 100% Resolution Uptime and Light Aura Detonations.

Creating Light Aura
  • Skill Combos: Leap through Light Fields (e.g., Greatsword 3 Leap of Faith inside Greatsword 4 Symbol of Resolution).
  • Stances & Virtues: Radiant Justice (F1), Radiant Resolve (F2), Effulgent Stance, or entering Radiant Forge (F4).
Detonating Light Aura
  • Stance Explosions: Cast Luminary Stance skills (Piercing Stance, Effulgent Stance, Daring Advance) to detonate active Light Auras via Sovereign of Light for massive AOE damage spikes.
