FIELD BRIEFING // TRAITS, HERO POINTS & TEMPLATES

CONFIGURE YOUR COMBAT IDENTITY, ADVENTURER

A weapon without a focused build is merely iron and wood. In Tyria, your traits and specializations determine how your skills synergize, turning basic attacks into devastating combos or party-wide support.

Use this field manual to collect Hero Points efficiently, unlock core specialization lines, and configure build templates for seamless hot-swapping in the field.

MODULE 1: HERO POINT ACQUISITION ▼

Unlocking Specializations

Hero Points (HPs) are used in the Training tab to unlock utility skills and core trait trees. Leveling up to 80 provides all needed points for core trees, but map challenges speed up your progression.

MODULE 2: TRAIT LINE SYNERGY & SPECIALIZATIONS ▼

Build Architecture

Characters can equip up to 3 Specializations simultaneously. Each specialization offers Minor (automatic) and Major (selectable) traits that modify how your skills perform.

MODULE 3: BUILD & EQUIPMENT TEMPLATES ▼

Hot-Swapping Loadouts

Build and Equipment Templates allow you to store different trait layouts and gear sets to swap instantly when out of combat.
