Traits & Templates

Your build doesn't need to be perfect. Start by understanding what your skills and traits are trying to do together.

Hero Points Traits Templates
At a Glance

What you're changing

Hero Points

Unlock your profession

Hero Points unlock profession skills and specializations as your character grows.

Traits

Change how things work

Traits modify your profession, reward certain actions, and help different parts of your build work together.

Templates

Save different setups

Templates let you keep different builds and equipment setups without rebuilding your character every time.

Core Lesson

Read the build before you copy the build

It's easy to find a build online and copy every trait without knowing what any of it does. That works until something changes or you want to adjust the build for yourself.

Start smaller. Read your weapon skills, profession skills, and traits. Look for ideas that keep showing up.

Critical Hits Burning Bleeding Healing Boons Blocking

Those repeated ideas are clues. They're showing you what your profession and current build are good at.

01 // Hero Points

You can't permanently mess this up

Hero Points are spent in the Training section of your Hero panel to unlock profession skills and specializations.

By the time you reach level 80, leveling alone gives you enough Hero Points to fully unlock your profession's core skills and specializations.

Central Tyria
1 HP

Most Hero Challenges in the core maps award one Hero Point.

Expansion Maps
10 HP

Expansion Hero Challenges generally award ten Hero Points and are the faster way to train elite specializations.

Don't Stress

Spend your Hero Points while leveling. You're not choosing one permanent path and locking yourself out of everything else.

02 // Specializations & Traits

Three lines shape the build

Your build can equip three specialization lines at once. Each line comes with traits that change or improve parts of your profession.

Adept

Choose one major trait from the first tier.

Master

Choose one major trait from the middle tier.

Grandmaster

Choose one major trait from the final tier.

With three specialization lines equipped, that gives you nine selected major traits across the build.

Veteran Field Note

Look for repeated words

You don't need to understand every number in a tooltip. Start by noticing which ideas keep appearing.

Your skills keep saying:

Burning, bleeding, torment, poison...

Your build may naturally be leaning toward condition damage.

Your traits keep saying:

Critical hits, strike damage, fury, might...

Your build may naturally be leaning toward direct power damage.

You don't need to name your build yet. You're just learning to read what it's telling you.

03 // Templates

Build and gear are saved separately

Guild Wars 2 gives you separate templates for your build and your equipment. That sounds more complicated than it really is.

Build Template

How your character plays

Build Templates store things such as your specialization lines, selected traits, and equipped slot skills.

Think:

“Which skills and traits am I using?”

Equipment Template

What your character is wearing

Equipment Templates hold your equipped weapons, armor, upgrades, and other equipment choices.

Think:

“Which gear am I using?”

You can switch templates from the Hero panel, the skill-bar template controls, or keybinds. The game limits swapping in certain combat or competitive situations.

Why Use More Than One?

Different situations can want different tools

You don't need separate builds when you're just starting out. But templates become useful once you want your character to do more than one job.

Open World

Maybe you prefer extra survivability, mobility, or self-boons while running around alone.

Group Content

In a group, you may want more damage, healing, boons, crowd control, or another job your team needs.

Experimenting

Keep your familiar setup saved while using another template to try something completely different.

Try It in Tyria

Read your own character

You don't need to rebuild anything. Just spend a couple minutes looking at what you already have.

Read

Pick one weapon and read its five skills. Look for words or effects that keep showing up.

Compare

Open your traits and see whether any of them improve the things your weapon or profession already does.

Explore

Look at your Build and Equipment Template tabs so you know where they are when you eventually want another setup.
