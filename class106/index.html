Lesson Goal

[LESSON 6 OF 6]

Configure your character's combat identity, Adventurer. A weapon without a synergistic build is merely iron and wood. This lesson covers gathering Hero Points efficiently, aligning trait trees with your primary stats, and setting up build templates for hot-swapping loadouts.

Step 01

Hero Point Acquisition

Hero Points (HPs) unlock utility skills and specialization trees. Leveling to 80 provides enough points to max all core trees, but completing map Hero Challenges grants points early.

Central Tyria = +1 HP | Expansions = +10 HP
Step 02

Trait Line Synergy

Equip up to 3 Specializations. Select 1 Major Trait per tier (Adept, Master, Grandmaster). Match trait effects to your damage type (Power, Condition, or Support).

3 Specializations | 9 Active Major Traits
Step 03

Build & Gear Templates

Store separate build layouts in your Hero Panel (H key) for Open World exploration versus Group Instanced content. Assign hotkeys to swap instantly out of combat.

Hero Panel (H) → Hot-Swap Out of Combat

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Open your Training tab (H) and ensure all core skill and specialization wheels are 100% unlocked.
  • Verify that all 9 Major Trait slots across your 3 equipped specialization lines are active and aligned with your main stats.
  • Switch between Build Template 1 and Build Template 2 while out of combat to test hotkey swapping.
