Advanced Guide // 201

Boons & Breakbars

Two bits of Guild Wars 2 combat language you'll hear constantly: keep the good buffs up, and break the blue bar.

Boons Crowd Control Breakbars
At a Glance

The short version

Boons

Good effects on you

Boons make you stronger, faster, tougher, or harder to control. Groups try to keep useful boons active during fights.

Breakbar

Blue means CC

When the bar under an enemy's health turns teal or blue, use crowd-control skills to damage it.

Group Play

Stay near your people

Many support skills affect nearby allies. Standing with your group makes it much easier to share boons, healing, and other support.

Core Lesson

Read the fight before chasing numbers

You don't need to memorize every boon icon or know the exact breakbar value of every skill.

Start with two habits: notice the buffs your group is giving you, and notice when an enemy's blue bar becomes active.

Once those two things start standing out automatically, the deeper mechanics make a lot more sense.

Part 1 // Boons

Buffs that help you and your group

Boons are positive effects. They appear as orange-colored icons in your active effects and can come from your own skills, traits, teammates, or other sources.

You don't need to recognize all twelve immediately. Start with the ones that noticeably change how combat feels.

Damage

Might, Fury, Quickness, and Alacrity are common offensive boons that help your group hit harder or use skills more efficiently.

Defense

Protection, Aegis, Stability, Resistance, and Resolution help reduce or avoid different kinds of incoming pressure.

Sustain & Movement

Regeneration restores health, Swiftness improves movement speed, and Vigor helps your endurance recover faster.

Boon Reference

What each one does

Use this as a reference. Nobody expects a new player to memorize the whole list on day one.

Aegis

Blocks the next incoming attack.

Alacrity

Makes your skills recharge faster.

Fury

Increases your critical-hit chance.

Might
UP TO 25

Increases your outgoing strike and condition damage.

Protection

Reduces incoming strike damage.

Quickness

Makes your skills and actions happen faster.

Regeneration

Restores health over time.

Resistance

Makes non-damaging conditions temporarily ineffective.

Resolution

Reduces incoming condition damage.

Stability
UP TO 25

Protects you from many control effects such as knockbacks, launches, stuns, and fears.

Swiftness

Increases your movement speed.

Vigor

Makes endurance regenerate faster so you can dodge more often.

How Boons Stack

More stacks or more time

Reapplying a boon usually does one of two things.

Intensity

Might & Stability

These can build multiple active stacks, up to 25.

Duration

Most other boons

Reapplying them generally keeps the same effect active for longer rather than making the effect stronger.

Don't Memorize the Caps Yet

There are duration caps and a few exceptions. That's useful later, but for now the important idea is simply: Might and Stability build stacks; most other boons build time.

Group Play

Why does everybody keep stacking together?

A lot of healing, boons, cleanses, and other support only reach nearby allies.

In organized squads, support is often arranged around smaller subgroups. Staying near your subgroup makes it much easier for everyone to receive the support meant for them.

Easy Version

Unless a mechanic tells you to spread out, being near your group is usually helpful.

Part 2 // Breakbars

See blue? Use crowd control.

Some stronger enemies have a Defiance Bar under their health bar. Players usually call it a breakbar.

Instead of being normally stunned, knocked down, or pushed around, these enemies use the bar to absorb crowd-control effects.

When the bar becomes active, your crowd-control skills damage that bar until the group breaks it.

Read the Bar

Three states you'll see

Teal // Active

Use CC now

Crowd-control effects can damage the bar. This is the moment players mean when they call for: “CC!” or “Break!”

Gray // Locked

Save your CC

The bar isn't vulnerable right now. Unless you need the skill for another reason, save your important crowd control for when the bar opens.

Brown // Recovering

The bar is resetting

The breakbar was recently depleted and is recovering. Wait for the next active window.

Crowd Control

What actually damages a breakbar?

Crowd control comes in two broad flavors. You don't need to memorize the exact breakbar damage of every skill yet.

Hard CC

Big chunks right away

These effects deal their breakbar damage immediately.

Stun Daze Knockdown Knockback Pull Launch Fear Taunt
Soft CC

Pressure over time

Some conditions damage an active breakbar over time instead of taking out one large chunk instantly.

Immobilize Chilled Slow Crippled Blinded
Break Successful

Why everyone gets excited when the bar disappears

Breaking a defiance bar often interrupts what the enemy was doing. Depending on the encounter, it may also stun or knock down the enemy and create a short window where the group can safely attack.

Many enemies also become Exposed after their bar is broken, which makes them take extra damage for a short time.

Veteran Field Note

Know which buttons are your CC buttons

You don't need to know every profession's crowd control. You should know your own.

Read your skill tooltips and look for words like Stun, Daze, Knockdown, Knockback, Pull, Launch, Fear, or Defiance Break.

When somebody calls “CC!”, those are the buttons you're looking for.

Try It in Tyria

Learn what to look for

No checklist. Just notice these things the next time you're in a group fight.

Boons

Watch your active effects during a group event and see how many boons appear when everyone stacks together.

Breakbar

Find a champion or boss with a defiance bar and notice when it changes from locked to active.

CC

Identify two or three skills on your own character that can help damage a breakbar.
