Lesson Goal

[LESSON 1 OF 6]

Boons are helpful status effects that give your team superpowers, like dealing more damage, recharging skills faster, or blocking hits. Think of them like temporary team armor. Learning how boons work and how they share with friends keeps your squad alive and strong.

Module 01

Screen Icons & UI

Active boons sit in the top row above your health orb as orange icons. A white ring around the icon shows how much time is left. If an icon starts flashing, it will end in 5 seconds.

Top Row = Boons | Bottom Row = Conditions
Module 02

How Boons Stack

Applying a boon twice makes it stronger. Might and Stability stack up to 25 times to multiply their power. All other boons add more time (usually capped at 30 seconds).

Might/Stability = Stacks | Others = More Time
Module 03

Who Gets Your Boons?

When you give boons, the game picks who gets them in order:
1. Your 5-person sub-team first
2. Other squad members next
3. Nearby players and pets last
Closer players get priority!

Subgroup First → Nearest Squad → Other Allies

Quick Boon Guide & Enemy Conversions

12 Primary Boons

Enemies can gain boons too! Some skills strip enemy boons or flip them into bad conditions (and vice versa for allies).

Aegis Stack: Time

Blocks the next incoming hit completely.

Flips to: Burning
Alacrity Stack: Time

Makes all skill cooldowns recharge faster.

Flips to: Chilled
Fury Stack: Time

Boosts your critical hit chance (+25%).

Flips to: Blinded
Might Stack: Power (Max 25)

Increases strike damage and condition damage.

Flips to: Weakness
Protection Stack: Time

Reduces incoming strike damage by 33%.

Flips to: Vulnerability
Quickness Stack: Time

Makes your skills and weapon attacks cast faster.

Flips to: Slow
Regeneration Stack: Time

Heals health every second.

Flips to: Poisoned
Resistance Stack: Time

Temporarily pauses non-damaging conditions (like chill or cripple).

Flips to: Chilled
Resolution Stack: Time

Reduces incoming condition damage by 33%.

Flips to: Confusion
Stability Stack: Power (Max 25)

Stops knockbacks, stuns, dazes, and fears from pushing you.

Flips to: Fear
Swiftness Stack: Time

Increases movement speed on foot by 33%.

Flips to: Crippled
Vigor Stack: Time

Increases dodge endurance recovery by 50%.

Flips to: Bleeding

Field Practice Checklist
