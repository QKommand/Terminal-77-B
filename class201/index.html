Lesson Goal

[LESSON 1 OF 6]

Master team buffs and boss crowd control. Boons give your squad powers like extra damage and speed. Defiance bars (breakbars) protect bosses from stuns—breaking a boss bar stops their big attacks and makes them take extra damage.

/// Part 1: Team Boons & Subgroups

Module 01

Boon Icons & Display

Active boons sit in the top row above your health orb as orange icons. A white outline ring shows time remaining. Icons flash when 5 seconds remain.

Top Row = Boons | Bottom Row = Conditions
Module 02

How Boons Stack

Applying a boon twice makes it stronger. Might and Stability stack power up to 25 stacks. All other boons add duration time (capped at 30s).

Might/Stability = Stacks | Others = Duration
Module 03

Subgroup Boon Priority

Skills prioritize your 5-person sub-team first, then nearby squad members, then other allies. Standing close to your subgroup ensures full boon coverage.

Subgroup First → Nearest Squad → Other Allies

/// Part 2: Defiance Bars (Breakbars) & Crowd Control

What Is a Breakbar?

[BOSS MECHANIC]

Champions and bosses have a bar located directly beneath their health bar called a Defiance Bar (Breakbar). This bar makes them immune to normal stuns and knockbacks. Players must hit the bar with Crowd Control (CC) skills to deplete it.

TEAL (Unlocked)

Hit it with CC skills now! Depleting the teal bar stops dangerous boss attacks, stuns the boss, and applies Exposed (+10% strike / +20% condition damage taken).

GRAY (Locked)

Completely immune to CC skills right now. Save your control skills until the bar turns teal during key attack phases.

BROWN (Recharging)

The bar was recently broken. The boss is recovering and immune to CC while the bar slowly refills back to full.

Instant Breakbar Damage

Hard Control Effects (Hard CC)

Hard CC skills immediately remove a large chunk of the teal breakbar in a single hit.

• Stun, Daze, Knockdown, Knockback
• Pull, Launch, Float, Sink, Fear, Taunt
Damage Over Time

Soft Control Effects (Soft CC)

Soft CC conditions deal continuous damage to the teal breakbar every second while active.

• Immobilize (Deals heavy bar damage over time)
• Chilled, Slow, Cripple, Blinded

Field Practice Checklist

[0 / 4 DONE]
  • Spot the top row of orange boon icons above your health globe during combat.
  • Stand close to your 5-person subgroup during a group meta to share boons cleanly.
  • Identify a boss's teal breakbar when they start charging a big attack.
  • Use a Hard CC skill (Stun, Knockdown, or Daze) on a teal breakbar to interrupt the boss.
