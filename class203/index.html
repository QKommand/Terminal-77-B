Lesson Goal

[ADVANCED TRACK]

Crafting turns raw materials (like wood, ore, and cloth) into gear, weapons, and food. Think of crafting like building with bricks. You gather raw parts, experiment at the bench to learn new models, and build strong gear for your account.

Module 01

Crafting Disciplines & Rules

Every character can maintain 2 active crafts at a time (up to 4 with licenses). Switch anytime for a small fee without losing progress. Homestead Handiworker is free and shared across your account.

Limit: 2 Active Disciplines (Max 500)
Module 02

Navigating the Crafting Bench

Use the Discovery tab to experiment with ingredients for new recipes, Production for known items, and direct access to your Bank and Material Storage vaults.

Tabs: Discovery | Production | Storage
Module 03

Material Tiers (T1 to T7)

Higher-level equipment requires higher tier materials. From Tier 1 (Level 1) up to Tier 6 and ascended Tier 7 at maximum discipline rank 500.

Tiers: T1 (Lvl 1) up to T7 (Lvl 500)
Module 04

Discovery & Time Gates

Combine ingredients in the Discovery tab to permanently learn recipes. High-tier items feature daily time-gated limits resetting at server reset.

Features: Discovery Tab | Daily Resets

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Pick Two Disciplines: Talk to a Master Craftsman in any major city (like Lion's Arch) and learn two disciplines that match your main character's armor and weapon choices.
  • Deposit Your Materials: Press the Deposit All Materials button in your inventory so your crafting items go straight to your Material Storage.
  • Discover Your First Item: Open the Discovery Tab at a crafting bench, drag matching components into the slots until the green craft button lights up, and create a new recipe.
