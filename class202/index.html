Lesson Goal

Leading a group or typing in chat doesn't have to be stressful. Turning on a group tag is basically like holding up a flashlight on a night tour—you don't need to know every single thing in the game, you just give people a clear spot to gather around.

Module 01

Group Icons (Tags)

Turning on an icon over your head (like the Apple or Commander symbol) acts like a beacon. Other players will naturally run over to help you with events and big bosses.

Key: Icons bring people together
Module 02

Sharing Links in Chat

Linking a map spot is like sending a pin in a text message. Press Ctrl + Click to send it right away, or Shift + Click to add words to your message first.

Key: Ctrl sends fast, Shift adds text
Module 03

Gathering Up

Standing right next to your team is like crowding under a giant umbrella in the rain. It makes sure all the heals, shields, and speed boosts reach you automatically.

Key: Stay close to get team buffs
Module 04

In-Game Search (/wiki)

If you don't know what an item is or how a quest works, type /wiki into chat and click the item. The game will open the answer page in your browser.

Key: /wiki looks up any item for you

Everyday Communication Tips

Simple tricks that make playing in groups much easier.

1. Map Spots & Item Links

Ctrl + Left Click: Instantly posts a map spot, piece of gear, or skill directly into chat.

Shift + Left Click: Drops the link into your chat box so you can type a message around it (like "Where do I use [Item]?").

2. Showing Your Gear

If a group asks you to "ping gear," they just want to make sure you have max-level armor equipped.

Open your Hero screen (H key), hold Shift + Left Click on your chest piece, and press Enter in chat.

3. Quick Lookups

Got weird items in your inventory or stuck on an event?

• Type /wiki and Shift + Click an Item to open its page.
• Type /wiki et to see event clock schedules.

Quick Practice Drills

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Link an Armor Piece: Open your Hero screen (H key), hold Shift, and click your armor to put its link into your chat box.
  • Look Up an Item: Type /wiki into chat, Shift + Click any item from your inventory, and hit Enter to open its page in your browser.
  • Open Event Timers: Type /wiki et into chat and press Enter to launch the live event schedule.
