Deep Dive: Chat Linking Essentials & Practical Uses

Why learning to work with chat links changes how you play.

1. Instant Link vs. Text Link

Ctrl + Click: Instantly fires the linked item, waypoint, or skill directly into your current active chat channel.

Shift + Click: Places the clickable link code into your open chat input bar without sending it, letting you type messages around it.

2. Raid / Strike LFG Verification

High-tier group leaders often ask joiners to "ping KP" (Kill Proof tokens) or "ping armor piece" to confirm you have appropriate Level 80 gear.

Open your hero panel or inventory, hold Shift + Click your piece of gear, and hit Enter in squad chat.

3. The /wiki Link Processor

Stuck on a quest item, unknown material, or map point? Open chat, type /wiki (with a space), then Shift + Click the item or waypoint into chat and press Enter.

The game automatically opens your web browser straight to that exact object's wiki page! Shortcut: type /wiki et for Event Timers.

Practice Drill Checklist

  • Ping an Equipped Item: Open your Hero Panel (H), press Shift + Left Click on your equipped chest piece to drop its link into chat, and send it to test gear pinging.

  • Check Live Event Timers: Open chat, type /wiki et, and press Enter to launch the live open-world event schedule.
