Lesson Goal

[ADVANCED TRACK]

Learn how to lead large groups without stress. Think of a Commander Tag like a flashlight in a dark room—wherever you go, 50 players will follow. This course teaches you how to organize your squad, drop clear ground markers, and keep everyone stacked together during big open-world fights.

Module 01

Squad Creation & Subgroups

Turn on your Commander Tag, make your group visible on the LFG menu, and sort players into 5-person subgroups so boons stay shared with their teammates.

Key: Drag players into balanced sub-teams
Module 02

Markers & Broadcasts

Drop colored ground symbols (Arrow, Circle, Heart) to show positioning, place target icons on key enemies, and use /sb for big on-screen announcements.

Key: Use /sb for vital direction cues
Module 03

Positioning & Stacking

Teach players to stack directly on your tag so healers and boon providers hit everyone. Use corners for line-of-sight pulls and lead safe movement loops around danger zones.

Key: Stop moving when group needs to fight
Module 04

Open-World Runs

Check map timers to arrive before metas start, assign trusted lieutenants to help guide members, and post clear waypoint links in map chat to build full squads fast.

Key: Share waypoint links in /map chat

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Create & Organize a Squad: Create a squad, post it on the LFG menu, and sort members into 5-person subgroups.
  • Set Ground Markers: Practice dropping Arrow, Circle, and Heart markers on the ground using hotkeys.
  • Send a Squad Broadcast: Type /sb Stack on Tag for Might! to send a clear on-screen message to your team.
