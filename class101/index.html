Keybinds & Movement

Before anything else in Guild Wars 2, you need to be able to move comfortably. This is your foundation for everything that comes after.

Core Lesson

Movement is the real skill check

You can have perfect gear and still struggle if you can't move well. Learn to strafe, dodge, and reposition without thinking about it.

Most combat in Guild Wars 2 is about staying alive while still dealing damage. Standing still is almost always a mistake.

01 // Camera Control

Your eyes are a weapon

Keep your camera moving. Always know what's around you, not just what's in front.

02 // Dodge Timing

Don't waste endurance

Dodging too early is the same as not dodging at all. Wait for the actual danger.

Veteran Tip

Rebind everything you hate

If a key feels awkward, change it. There is no “correct” layout—only what your hands can execute quickly.
