Lesson Goal

[LESSON 1 OF 6]

Good movement is like walking on solid ground while others slip on ice. Setting up easy to use movement keys makes dodging attacks much easier and keeps your eyes on the battlefield.

Step 01

Strafe Controls

Strafing will be used as a combat mechanic with some enemies. Clicking and holding the right button on your mouse will control the camera.

A = Strafe Left | D = Strafe Right
Step 02

Quick Dodge Button

Turn off double-tap direction keys to dodge. Map dodge to a single button sucy as the C key to stop accidental wipes.

Key Options: Mouse 4 or C Key
Step 03

Class Skill Keys

Move F1 through F5 skill keys closer to your movement fingers. Easy choices are Shift + 1-4, Q, E, or mouse buttons.

Target Keys: Q, E, R, Mouse 5

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Circle-strafe a target training dummy while holding right click on your mouse.
  • Dodge sideways through an enemy red attack ring using your new single dodge button.
  • Use class skills (F1-F5) without looking down at your keyboard.
