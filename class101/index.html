WELCOME TO TYRIA, ADVENTURER

You step into a continent shaped by centuries of magic, ancient wars, and the lingering scars of Elder Dragons. Tyria is a living, unpredictable world that does not wait for you to take turns.

In Tyria, survival is claimed through swift movement, spatial awareness, and muscle memory. Use this field manual to calibrate your interface, know your weapon reach, and test what configuration feels natural to you in battle.
