Field Archive // 101

Keybinds & Movement

Get your controls comfortable enough that you can watch the fight instead of fighting your keyboard.

Movement Dodging Keybinds
At a Glance

What matters here

Move

Stay mobile

Guild Wars 2 lets you move while using most skills. Good movement can keep you out of danger without stopping what you're doing.

Dodge

Save it for when you need it

Dodging gives you a short evade window, but it costs endurance. You don't need to dodge every attack you see.

Comfort

Your binds are yours

There isn't one perfect control setup. The goal is to reach the skills you use often without awkward stretching or looking down.

Core Lesson

Get comfortable before you get clever

Good controls aren't about copying somebody else's keyboard layout. They're about making movement and your most-used skills feel natural. If you're constantly reaching across the keyboard or looking down to find a button, move that button somewhere easier.

Think of your controls like arranging tools on a workbench. The ones you grab all the time should be close. The ones you rarely touch can live farther away.

01 // Movement

Strafe instead of turning

Strafing lets you move sideways while keeping your character and camera pointed toward the fight. Many players bind A and D to strafe instead of keyboard turning.

Holding the right mouse button also lets you steer the camera and quickly change direction while moving.

Common setup: A = Strafe Left // D = Strafe Right
02 // Dodge

Give dodge its own button

Double-tap dodge works, but it can also cause accidental dodges when you're trying to make small movement adjustments or move near an edge.

A dedicated dodge button gives you more control. Pick something that's easy for your hand to reach without disrupting movement.

Examples: Mouse 4 // C // V // another comfortable key
03 // Profession Skills

Bring important skills closer

Profession mechanics often live on F1-F5. Depending on your keyboard and profession, those keys may be perfectly comfortable or annoyingly far away.

If you're stretching for them, move frequently used profession skills closer to your movement hand or mouse.

Options: Q // E // R // Shift + keys // mouse side buttons
Veteran Field Note

Don't dodge what you can walk out of

A lot of attacks give you enough time to simply move out of the way. If walking or strafing gets you somewhere safe, save your endurance. That leaves your dodge ready for the attack that actually needs it.

Early on, the goal isn't perfect movement. Start noticing the difference between “I can move” and “I need to dodge.”

Try It in Tyria

A few things worth practicing

No checklist. Just try these the next time you're somewhere safe enough to experiment.

Move

Circle around an enemy or training target while keeping your camera pointed toward it.

Compare

Walk out of one enemy attack, then use a dodge on another and notice how each option feels.

Adjust

Use your profession mechanic a few times. If you have to look down or stretch awkwardly, try a different bind.
