PACK YOUR RUCK SACK, ADVENTURER

A campaign in Tyria is won as much in the rucksack as it is on the battlefield. An Adventurer whose bags are choked with unrefined ores, junk trophies, and unorganized gear will spend more time standing motionless before vendors than defeating Elder Dragon minions.

Use this field manual to configure your bag architecture, automate material deposits, establish salvaging routines, and keep your inventory clear during long operations.

MODULE 1: BAG ARCHITECTURE & SLOT TYPES ▼

Tactical Setup

Specialized containers automatically filter loot as you pick it up in the field. Equipping the right mix of bags eliminates manual sorting.

MODULE 2: THE MATERIAL VAULT & DEPOSIT MECHANICS ▼

Remote Storage Protocol

Crafting components do not need to sit in your active rucksack. Offload them instantly from anywhere in Tyria directly into your Account Bank.

MODULE 3: SALVAGE PROTOCOLS & RARITY RULES ▼

Resource Extraction

Breaking down surplus weapons and armor provides raw materials and Luck Essences. Matching the right salvage kit to item rarity maximizes profit.

MODULE 4: LIQUIDATION & COMMERCE ▼

Field Monetization

Convert useless trophies and excess material stacks into gold efficiently without clogging your workspace.
