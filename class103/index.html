Lesson Goal

[LESSON 3 OF 6]

A campaign in Tyria is won as much in the rucksack as it is on the battlefield. An Adventurer whose bags are choked with unrefined ores, junk trophies, and unorganized gear will spend more time standing motionless before vendors than defeating tough enemies. Configure your bag layout, automate material deposits, establish salvaging routines, and keep your inventory clear during long operations in the field.

Step 01

Bag Architecture & Slot Types

Equip an Invisible Bag or Safe Box in your bottom bag slot to protect gear sets, food, and weapons from "Salvage All" and vendor bulk sales. Use specialized containers (Craftsman's Boxes, Oresman's Bags) to auto-group incoming raw materials, and upgrade default starter bags to 18-slot or 20-slot bags.

Invisible Bag = Safe Slot | 18-20 Slot Bags
Step 02

Material Vault & Deposit Mechanics

Crafting components do not need to sit in your active rucksack. Locate the "Deposit All Materials" button (Gear Icon in top-right inventory) to offload crafting items instantly into your Account Bank from anywhere in Tyria. Monitor your default 250-stack material limit so overflowing materials can be listed on the Trading Post.

Deposit All (Gear Icon) | 250 Stack Cap
Step 03

Salvage Protocols & Liquidation

Breaking down surplus gear provides materials and Luck Essences. Use Basic Salvage Kits (or Copper-Fed tools) for Fine (Blue) and Masterwork (Green) items. Reserve Master's, Mystic, or Silver-Fed Kits for Rare (Yellow) and Exotic (Orange) gear to guarantee higher Glob of Ectoplasm recovery. Stack Unidentified Gear instead of opening items immediately during long metas.

Blue/Green = Basic | Rare/Exotic = Master's

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Equip at least one Invisible Bag / Safe Box in your lowest bag slot to safeguard weapons, food, and gear swap sets.
  • Use the "Deposit All Materials" button via the Inventory gear menu while in the open world to clear crafting components instantly.
  • Salvage Fine/Masterwork items with a Basic Kit, use a Master's/Mystic Kit on Rare items, and sell junk items at the nearest vendor.
