Veteran Quality-of-Life Secret Reference

Essential shortcuts used by seasoned Tyrian commanders.

The Wiki Command

Type /wiki into chat, shift-left-click any item or waypoint, and press Enter to open the official wiki instantly.

Claim Bouncy Chests

Right-click any bouncy chest popup in the bottom-right corner of your screen to claim all rewards instantly without clicking them individually.

Action Camera Mode

Bind a key in Control Options to "Toggle Action Camera" for a free-aim third-person reticle combat experience.

Deposit All & Compact

Click the gear icon in your top-right inventory window to instantly trigger Deposit All Materials and clean up your bags.

Looking For Group (Y Key)

Open the LFG menu to find active squads running meta events, world bosses, and dungeon trains across every zone.

Pinging Waypoints in Chat

Type /m Need help! and shift-click the nearest blue map diamond so allies can jump straight to your location.
