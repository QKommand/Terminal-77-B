Lesson Goal

[LESSON 4 OF 6]

Navigate Tyria with speed. Crossing entire regions on foot will leave you stranded far behind when open-world meta events and squad commanders move across the map. This lesson covers fast travel waypoints, movement speed boosts, and the specialized controls of ground, water, and aerial mounts.

Step 01

Waypoints & Speed Boosts

Waypoints provide instant map travel for a minor fee. On foot, Swiftness boosts movement speed by +33%, while Superspeed caps your mobility at the maximum +100% threshold during active combat mechanics.

Swiftness = +33% | Superspeed = +100%
Step 02

Ground Mount Skills

Raptors leap long horizontal gaps over ravines. Springers charge high vertical jumps up cliff faces. Jackals evade-blink through sand portals, while Warclaws dash across rugged battlefields.

Raptor = Long | Springer = High | Jackal = Blink
Step 03

Water & Aerial Flight Controls

Skimmers hover over open water, sulfur, and hazard floors. Griffons dive to build massive airspeed across open valleys. Skyscales hover stationary in 3D space and cling to walls to climb ledges.

Skimmer = Water | Griffon = Speed | Skyscale = Hover

Practice Drill Checklist

[0 / 3 DONE]
  • Set up distinct, comfortable keybinds for your primary ground and flying mounts in Control Options.
  • Practice long distance leaps over river gaps with the Raptor and high vertical jumps with the Springer.
  • Test hovering over open water with a Skimmer or maintaining stationary flight altitude on a Skyscale.
