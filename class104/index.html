FIELD BRIEFING // MAP TRAVEL & MOUNT CONTROLS

NAVIGATE TYRIA WITH SPEED, ADVENTURER

Crossing entire regions on foot will leave you stranded far behind when open-world meta events and squad commanders move across the map. Master fast travel waypoints, movement speed boosts, and the specialized abilities of ground, water, and aerial mounts.

Use this field manual to set up dedicated mount keybinds, understand movement speed limits, and execute tactical dismounts in active battle zones.

MODULE 1: WAYPOINTS & MOVEMENT SPEED BOOSTS ▼

Fast Travel & Speed Caps

Waypoints provide instant map travel for a minor copper fee. On foot, movement speed is dictated by boon application and character traits.

MODULE 2: GROUND MOUNT MECHANICS ▼

Terrain Traversal

Ground mounts bypass environmental barriers through unique movement skills. Matching the correct mount to the terrain prevents fall damage and dead ends.

MODULE 3: WATER & AERIAL FLIGHT CONTROLS ▼

3D Navigation & Pitch Controls

Water and flying mounts require controlling altitude and pitch momentum. Hovering and diving mechanics allow seamless travel over hazardous hazards.

MODULE 4: DRILLS & ERGONOMIC CONTROL CONFIGURATION ▼

Control Customization

Fumbling through drop-down menus to summon mounts in combat will slow you down. Rebind specific mounts to individual keys or radial wheels.
