Travel & Mounts

Tyria is huge. Learn the easy ways to get around so you spend less time running back to places you've already been.

Waypoints Mounts Movement
At a Glance

Your travel toolbox

Waypoints

Fast travel

Once you've unlocked a waypoint, you can travel back to it from your map for a small fee.

Mounts

Different tools for different terrain

Mounts aren't just faster running. Each one handles movement in a different way.

On Foot

You still need your feet

Swiftness and other movement bonuses help when you're not mounted or when combat keeps you on foot.

Core Lesson

Use the right shortcut for the situation

Travel in Guild Wars 2 is less about finding one perfect mount and more about knowing your options.

A nearby waypoint might be faster than crossing half the map. A Raptor can clear a gap. A Springer can get you up a ledge. A Skimmer makes water much less annoying.

Think of mounts like tools in a backpack. You don't need every tool for every job.

01 // Waypoints

The easiest shortcut in Tyria

Once a waypoint has been unlocked by that character, open the world map and select it to travel there.

Normal waypoint travel has a small coin cost based partly on how far you're traveling. Travel between waypoints inside major cities is free. ([Guild Wars 2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Waypoint?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Good Habit

If you're already passing a waypoint, grab it. Even if you don't need it now, it may save you a long trip later.

02 // On Foot

Swiftness is your basic speed boost

Swiftness increases movement speed by 33%. Many professions can provide it to themselves or nearby allies. ([Guild Wars 2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Swiftness?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Superspeed is a stronger movement effect in combat, but movement speed has caps and the exact result changes depending on whether you're in or out of combat. You don't need to memorize the math. ([Guild Wars 2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Movement_Speed?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Easy Version

Swiftness = faster. Superspeed = much faster when it matters.

Mount Basics

Each mount has a job

Guild Wars 2 mounts are designed around different types of movement, not just different skins. You don't need to unlock all of them before you can enjoy the game. ([Guild Wars 2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Mount?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Raptor

Distance

Fast ground travel with a long forward leap. Great for covering distance and clearing gaps.

Springer

Height

Built around vertical jumping. Useful when the place you need to reach is above you instead of across from you.

Skimmer

Water

Glides quickly over water and other difficult surfaces, making watery maps much easier to cross.

Jackal

Short bursts

Uses quick movement bursts and can interact with special sand portals once you've unlocked the related mastery.

Griffon

Airspeed

More glider than helicopter. It turns height into speed and can cross huge distances when you learn how to dive and climb.

Skyscale

Precision

Hovers, climbs, and gives you a lot of control in the air. It's especially useful when you need careful movement instead of raw speed.

There are other mounts too, including the Roller Beetle, Warclaw, and Siege Turtle. You'll meet them as you get farther into the game. ([Guild Wars 2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Mount?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Controls

Give your favorite mounts easy buttons

Opening the mount menu every time works, but dedicated mount keybinds make swapping much quicker.

You don't have to bind every mount immediately. Start with the two or three you use most and put them somewhere comfortable.

Example

You might keep Raptor, Skimmer, and your favorite flying mount on easy keys, then use the mount menu for everything else.

Veteran Field Note

Don't automatically mount for every ten-foot trip

Mounts are great, but learning the map still matters. Sometimes a waypoint, a short walk, or simply following the terrain is faster than constantly mounting and dismounting.

And remember: you normally can't mount while you're in combat, so knowing how to move well on foot still matters. ([Guild Wars 2 Wiki](https://wiki.guildwars2.com/wiki/Dismount?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Try It in Tyria

Take the long way once

Pick somewhere nearby and experiment with the different ways you can reach it.

Waypoint

Open your map and fast travel back to a waypoint you've already unlocked.

Mount

Try two different mounts on the same terrain and notice what each one makes easier.

Bind

Give your most-used mount an easy keybind and see whether it feels better than opening the mount menu.
